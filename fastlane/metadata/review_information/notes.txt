SIGN-IN
An account is required only to purchase. Browsing the catalogue and playing the free tours needs no account. To sign in: Me tab (bottom right) - Sign in - use the demo credentials above.

HOW TO TEST THE IN-APP PURCHASE ($0.99)
Tour Tier 0.99 is the only in-app purchase currently attached to any tour. The other tier products are price points for our creator-set pricing model; none is attached to a published tour yet, so they are not part of this submission and will be submitted as they come into use.
Almost all of Dozent's 1,550+ tours are free. The paid items are the 66 multi-stop walking tours, so please use the filter below or you are unlikely to encounter one:
1. On the Home screen, tap the PRICE chip in the filter row above the map, then choose PAID. The map and list now show only the paid tours, each $0.99 (a price badge appears on the card).
2. Tap any of them, for example "Fifth Avenue Walk" (New York).
3. On the tour page, tap the price/Buy button and complete the sandbox purchase.
4. The tour unlocks immediately and plays from that same page.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Accounts can be deleted inside the app: Me tab - Settings (gear icon) - Account - Delete account. To test it, please create a new account rather than deleting the demo login, so the demo account stays available for future reviews.

LOCATION IS NOT REQUIRED FOR REVIEW
Dozent uses GPS so each stop's audio starts automatically as you walk. You do not need to be on location: every tour can be played manually with the play button from anywhere.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT AND MODERATION
Signed-in users can author their own tours (Me tab - plus button). Submitted tours are NOT published automatically - each one is reviewed and approved by us before it can appear in the catalogue. Every tour also has a "Report a concern" item in its ... menu, which notifies us directly.

Thank you for reviewing Dozent.
