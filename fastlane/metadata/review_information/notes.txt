Thank you for reviewing Dozent.

WHAT THE APP DOES
Dozent is a catalogue of narrated walking tours of city landmarks. Each tour is
tied to a real-world location. When you physically arrive at a stop, the audio
for that stop begins automatically.

HOW TO TEST IT WITHOUT WALKING ANYWHERE
Because stops are triggered by arriving at a GPS location, waiting for one to
fire at your desk will not work. Two ways around that:

1. Simply press the play button on any tour. This begins playback immediately
   and does not depend on your location at all. This is the quickest way to see
   the whole experience - audio, photographs, transcript, lock-screen controls.

2. If you would like to see the automatic arrival behaviour, use a simulated
   location. Most tours in New York are within a short distance of each other;
   for example the Empire State Building tour is at 40.7484, -73.9857.

HOW TO TEST THE IN-APP PURCHASE ($0.99)
An account IS required to purchase (see the demo credentials supplied with this
submission). Browsing, playing free tours, downloading and saving all work
signed out; only buying needs an account. To sign in: Me tab (bottom right) ->
Sign in.

Almost all of the 1,350 tours are free. The paid items are the 66 multi-stop
walking tours, so please use the filter below or you are unlikely to encounter
one:

1. On the Home screen, tap the WALKS chip in the filter row above the map. The
   list now shows only multi-stop walks - these are the paid tours, each $0.99
   (a price badge appears on the card).
2. Tap any walk, for example "Fifth Avenue Walk" (New York).
3. On the tour page, tap the price/Buy button and complete the sandbox purchase.
4. The tour unlocks immediately and plays from that same page.

PERMISSIONS WE ASK FOR, AND WHY
- Location - to show tours near you on the map, and to start a stop's audio
  when you arrive at it. The app is fully usable if you decline: you can still
  browse, search, and press play on any tour manually.
- Background location - so a tour keeps advancing while your phone is in your
  pocket and the screen is off, which is the normal way this app is used.
- Microphone - only used if you choose to record your own narration when
  creating a tour. It is never accessed otherwise.
- Camera - only used to scan a join code when listening in a group with someone
  else. It is never accessed otherwise.

BACKGROUND AUDIO
The app declares the audio background mode because a tour is designed to keep
playing while you walk with the screen locked.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT AND MODERATION
Signed-in users can author their own tours (Me tab -> plus button). Submitted
tours are NOT published automatically - each one is reviewed and approved by us
before it can appear in the catalogue. Every tour also carries a "Report a
concern" item in its overflow menu, which notifies us directly.

CONTENT
First-party tours are written and produced in-house. Photographs are either
supplied by us or sourced from public-domain collections.
