Dozent turns a walk into a guided tour. The audio starts on its own the moment you reach a stop — no fumbling for the right track, no staring at a screen while the building is right there in front of you.

WHAT'S INSIDE
More than 1,100 tours across 24 cities — New York, London, Paris, Tokyo, Kyoto, Rome, Madrid, Amsterdam, Berlin, Hong Kong, Seoul, Rio de Janeiro and more. Every stop carries photographs and a full transcript, if you would rather read than listen.

TWO SHAPES OF TOUR
• Single pieces — one place, one short piece of audio. A few minutes, then back to your day.
• Walks — several stops strung together, each one starting by itself as you arrive. Prefer to pick your moment? Some tours play tap by tap instead.

WHAT YOU'LL FIND
• History — the stories behind the places
• Architecture — the buildings worth slowing down for
• Art — public works, in context
• Food and drink, parks, sacred sites, and the corners only locals know

HOW IT WORKS
Open the map, find something near you, press start, and put your phone in your pocket. Dozent watches for the moment you arrive at each stop and begins the audio then. Keep walking; it keeps up.

WORKS WITHOUT SIGNAL
Download a tour before you set off and it plays with no connection at all — no roaming charges abroad, and no dead air underground.

LISTEN TOGETHER
Walking with someone? Start a group and everyone hears the same sentence at the same moment, so nobody is a paragraph behind.

BUILT FOR WALKING
• Lock-screen and AirPods controls work end to end
• Audio keeps playing with the phone in your pocket
• Save what catches your eye into lists you can come back to
• Designed for iPhone, and at home on iPad and Apple Vision Pro

NO ADS, NO TRACKING
No advertising, no analytics, no tracking. An account is optional — browsing, playing and downloading all work without one. Signing in only adds syncing your library between your own devices.
