Pinned posts on the map. Alongside our own tours you will now find short videos from people who film the places they love — architecture, food, history — playing right inside Dozent. Hundreds of them, and more arriving every week.

Place pages. When several tours cover the same building or square, they now share one page, so everything about a place sits in one spot.

Expand any map. The small map on a tour, a place, a creator or one of your lists now has a button that opens the main map, already centred on what you were reading about.

Photographs work offline. Download a tour before you leave and its pictures come with it. Photos you have already seen keep showing even when the signal does not.

A faster start. The app now waits until it is genuinely ready, then arrives all at once instead of assembling itself in front of you.

Video, full screen. Tap a clip in a tour's gallery to fill the screen, and turn your phone for landscape footage.

Search is quick now. Results appear as you type instead of lagging behind, and tapping one takes you to that place on the map.

Also in this update: shared links show the tour instead of a blank card, saved lists can be viewed as a grid and sorted, and two tours that would not start where their own narration told you to stand have been put right.
