# Mercedes-Benz Museum: one post on two platforms, not a place

_opened 2026-09-15 · rewritten after the place decisions · clear with
`git rm status/owner/mercedes-benz-museum-archimarathon-dup.md`_

This was filed alongside Nishiki Market and Matcha Stand Ippuku as a
place candidate. Both of those became places; **this one deliberately did
not**, and the reason is worth keeping.

All three looked identical to `check-place-candidates.py` — two entries on
one coordinate. What separates them is **who posted them**:

| | creators | caption similarity | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Matcha Stand Ippuku | `@michitabi.tokyo` + `@camdyerjapan` | **10.6%** | place |
| Nishiki Market | Atlas KYO + `@revisit_japan` + `@nom_life` | independent | place |
| **Mercedes-Benz Museum** | **`@archimarathon` twice** | **83.8%** | **not a place** |

Both Mercedes entries are `@archimarathon`, the same handle on TikTok and
Instagram, carrying the same text down to "takes you on a winding journey
from top to bottom". It is **one post cross-posted**, not two entries
describing a site. A place page here would show a visitor the same video
twice and call it a collection.

🔴 **The coordinate check cannot see this.** Two pins on one point look the
same whether they are two creators or one creator twice. The discriminator
is the caption and the handle, and it has to be read.

**Your call, and it is a duplicate question rather than a places one:**
keep both (they reach different audiences, and the platforms are genuinely
separate) or drop one. Nothing is broken either way — both pins work, and
their heroes are distinct files.
