# Miami photos: most are Creative Commons, not public domain — keep with credits, or swap?

The Miami batch was reported as "all public domain, no credit owed". **That was wrong.**
Of the 77 photos picked from Wikimedia Commons, **at least 61 legally require a credit line**
(31 CC BY, 30 CC BY-SA). Only 3 are confirmed public domain; 13 could not be checked yet.

Nothing is exposed today: no Miami tour is live, and no catalogue entry uses these files.
But the app has no credits screen, so they cannot ship as they are.

Choose one:
1. **Keep them** and log each in `drafts/CREDITS.md`. They will then need a credits line or screen before Miami goes live.
2. **Swap them** for your own photos or Unsplash (no credit owed). Unsplash found nothing usable for Miami's named places, though.
3. **Mix:** keep CC photos only where nothing else exists.
