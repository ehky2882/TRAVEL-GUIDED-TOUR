**⚠️ Recorded retroactively on 2026-09-18 — and this row exists because of how it was found.**

**Rail placement and heading**, both owner decisions of 2026-09-17 after confirming the rail worked
on build 170. Placed **second, under "In view"/"Near you" and above the editorial shelves**: where
you are beats what you saved; what you saved beats the shelves.

**The heading names no tour, and that is measured rather than stylistic.** The rail title renders in
all-caps SF Mono, ~32 characters to a line. *"BECAUSE YOU SAVED "* alone eats 18, leaving 14 for a
title whose median is 22 and maximum 86 — so **only 22% of titles fit on one line**. The shortest
sensible prefix reached 67%; cutting titles at their natural break got to 73%. **No wording can
guarantee it**, and the owner wanted neither a two-line heading nor an ellipsis — so it reads
*"More like what you saved"*, 24 characters, always one line. That also let the rail take a stable
id rather than one built from the seed's uuid, so it keeps its view identity when the seed changes.
