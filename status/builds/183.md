# Build 183 — Onboarding round 2: real accounts, Reels-style taps, Skip skips only sign-up (PR #1093)

_2026-10-25 17:33 UTC_

WHAT CHANGED (onboarding + the five Home guides)
- Screen 3: new definition; the made-up example sentence is gone.
- Reels-style navigation everywhere: tap the left third to go back, anywhere else to go on; swipe either way. Buttons kept.
- Skip skips only sign-up — you carry on at "Welcome." Tapping through the account screens counts as Skip.
- Sign-up form: every field required (brass *), now with an Email field.
- Accounts are REAL: "Create an account" with Apple, Google or Email makes a real account and saves your name and username. "I already have an account" opens the real Sign in.
- Six dozents: Follow really follows when signed in; no Follow buttons if you skipped.
- Guides: left tap goes back; back on the first guide returns to the last onboarding card. The list guide now lights the list, and the list always starts at half height.
