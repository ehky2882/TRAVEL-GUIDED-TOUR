🔴 **This is the build where the owner found a bug no automated check could have.** They tested with
tours already saved, saved a fresh one, and **no rail appeared**. The app stores saves in **two**
places — Liked (`LibraryStore`) and named lists (`TourListService`) — and the rail read only the
first. **Anyone who files tours into lists saw nothing at all.**

⚠️ **`SaveState.swift` exists to prevent exactly this and says so in its opening lines:** *"a tour is
saved when it belongs to at least one list … there is no separate saved flag living alongside list
membership — that split is exactly what this replaces."* The feature reimplemented the split that
file was written to remove, while `TourSaveActions.isSaved` already held the rule and is used by
every bookmark surface in the app.

**Seven tests passed because they all shared the wrong premise.** Volume of green felt like evidence
and was not. Fixed in 170.
