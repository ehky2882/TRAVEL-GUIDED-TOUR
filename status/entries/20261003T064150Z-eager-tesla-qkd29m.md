# SovMod launch — 72 original audio tours

_2026-10-03 06:41 UTC · branch `eager-tesla-qkd29m`_

**SovMod launch: 72 original audio tours, a new creator 🗿.** Socialist-era architecture and monuments across 21 countries (8 of them new to the catalogue). All singles are geofenced, priced free, and carry recorded audio plus 1–7 photos each (169 in all, royalty-free or public domain per the contributor).

- 8 supplied coordinates were wrong by 280 m to 1.8 km and were moved to their OpenStreetMap venues: Ugala, Avas, Chorsu, Costinești, Makedonium, the Tuzla bank, the Georgia–Russia monument and Dom-Korabl.
- Every MP3 was transcript-matched to its own script.
- Prypiat (exclusion zone, closed) and We Are Our Mountains (Khankendi) are kept at the contributor's request.
- Place question for Edward: Kosmaj, Avala and Genex sit on existing pins (`status/owner/place-sovmod-belgrade-trio.md`).
