⚠️ **Why the gap is worth one more test rather than a shrug.** The four safety rules refuse a merge
that **shrinks** the catalogue; **nothing refuses one that grows it.** Were ids ever compared
case-sensitively — pin ids are UPPERCASE in `Tours.json`, lowercase out of Postgres — the merge
would not throw. It would **append a duplicate of every changed pin and cache it**, and the count
would climb on every content change until somebody noticed. `CatalogDeltaMerge.identifier(of:)`
lowercases and a unit test covers it, but that is the one failure mode with no runtime guard
behind it, and it has never run against production.
