# Two All'Antico pins were 4.5 km from the branch they film

_2026-09-17 01:31 UTC · branch `allantico-sullivan-street-260917`_

Owner watched both videos: *"both All'Antico links are for sullivan street
location"*. That turned a naming question into a **coordinate defect**.

🔴 **Both pins were 4.54 km from the restaurant they film.** They sat at
`40.7636814, -73.9700441` — East 60th Street — while the branch in the videos is
**225 Sullivan Street**, Greenwich Village. Moved to `40.7295949, -73.9996291`,
which is OpenStreetMap's node **for the venue itself** (`fast_food`,
`All'Antico Vinaio, 225, Sullivan Street`), not a street-address centroid.
Verified by ward and postcode — Manhattan, 10012 — per rule 8d, not by distance.

**Place created: `All'Antico Vinaio on Sullivan Street`**, 2 members.

⚠️ **Named for the BRANCH, deliberately.** All'Antico Vinaio has four NYC
locations and the place id is `uuid5(city + name)`, so a place called simply
"All'Antico Vinaio" in New York would collide with the next branch to get pins.
The catalogue already carries a separate pin titled *"All'Antico Vinaio on
Eighth Avenue"* — that convention is followed rather than invented.

**How close this came to shipping wrong.** This session had proposed grouping
these two as the **Upper East Side** branch, inferred from the very coordinate
that turns out to be wrong. The owner asked *"is the all'antico video really
specifically about the ues location?"*, the honest answer was that the captions
never say, and watching them settled it. 🔴 **A wrong coordinate and a genuine
neighbour are indistinguishable from the catalogue alone** — `docs/places.md`
says exactly that, and this is the case it describes: the place would have been
minted on a 4.5 km error and every member dragged onto it.

⚠️ The two pins keep their caption titles (*"We have sorority girls to thank for
this one"*). Retitling them to the venue, as #976 did for Lupa Osteria, is a
separate call for the owner — the place page now carries the readable name.

**Unchanged and verified correct:** the Eighth Avenue pin at `40.759699,
-73.98822` (729 8th Ave), and the two Florence pins, which are already a place.
