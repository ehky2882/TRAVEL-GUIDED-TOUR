Ferrairo nineteen forty seven

The name over this door has nothing at all to do with chocolate hazelnut spread, which is the first thing worth clearing up.

Stop outside number fifteen on Corso Mattayottee, a broad avenue in the centre of town, where a showroom with Ferrairo nineteen forty seven above the door has big windows full of chairs, tables, sofas and lamps, arranged more like rooms than displays.

Take in those windows for a moment, because they show what the shop is really about. Ferrairo nineteen forty seven is a furniture and interior design business, founded in nineteen forty seven and still carrying the family name, and it deals in contemporary Italian and international design; it's a stockist for Kappelleenee, one of the Italian names that helped turn modern furniture into something people collect.

Look out, too, for pieces that don't come from the big brands. Since twenty fifteen the shop has been designing and producing its own furniture collection, led by the designer Looka Montrookyo, so some of what's in front of you was conceived close to here rather than in a factory catalogue.

And here's the reason the name is worth a second thought. Ferrairo the confectioner started in Alba in the nineteen forties too, and visitors understandably assume this is a chocolate shop, but this Ferrairo has spent nearly eighty years selling something else entirely: the idea that a sofa or a lamp can carry as much care and invention as anything you'd find in a gallery. It's a very Turin story, in its way, because this is a city that has long been as proud of its designers and engineers as of its sweets.

If you fancy more of the same, Turin's design shops are dotted all through the centre, and the streets around Piazza Solfereeno are a good place to keep looking; that broad square is only a short walk away.
