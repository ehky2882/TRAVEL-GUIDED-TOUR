Leebrereeah Internatsyonahleh Luxemburg

This bookshop has been selling books in Turin since eighteen seventy two, and in January twenty twenty five it moved house, about fifty metres down the street.

At the corner of Veeah Akkadaymya dellay Shentseh and Veeah Chayzareh Batteestee, the Leebrereeah Internatsyonahleh Luxemburg has two entrances: one on Veeah Batteestee, and another opening straight into the Gallereeah Soobalpeena, the arcade beside it. From the corner you'll see the windows stacked with books in several languages.

The history is the place to begin, because the shop started life as the Libreria Berf, a Turin branch of a Genoa bookseller, and later became the Luxemburg, an international bookshop where you could find newspapers and novels from across Europe. Antonio Peettarellee and Jeejee Rahyola have run it since two thousand and two.

Step inside to see the new rooms, where three spaces were joined together, more than two hundred square metres in all, and where the designers Barbara Brondi and Marco Raeenoh kept the old wooden shelving and set it against exposed brick vaults and dark walls, so the old shop came along and simply got more room.

Look for the counter at the centre, too, because there's a small cafe now, created with the ceramics firm Mooteena and the historic Turin coffee house Barahttee and Meelahno, with a ceramic counter by the designers Ronan and Erwan Booroolek.

Seen from the street, this is simply a bookshop that moved fifty metres, but seen from inside it's a small piece of the city's civic memory. It helped launch Turin's international book fair, and in the nineteen seventies it was connected with Fworee, one of Italy's first gay rights movements, founded by Anjelo Pettsahna; art, design and el jee bee tee queue literature remain among its specialities today.

When you've finished browsing, take the door into the Gallereeah Soobalpeena and walk through towards Piazza Kastello. On the way you'll pass an antiquarian bookshop whose walls are hung with old maps, which makes a rather nice pair with this one.
