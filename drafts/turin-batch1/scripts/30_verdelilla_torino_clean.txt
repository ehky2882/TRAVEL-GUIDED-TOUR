Verdelilla Torino

This boutique is stocked by people who've spent their working lives in fashion, and it's built around the idea that a shop should feel like being dressed by a friend with very good taste.

Look for number seventeen on Via Assietta, a quiet street of elegant apartment buildings close to Porta Nuova, where Verdelilla's windows show a carefully chosen handful of clothes, bags and shoes rather than a crowded display. Inside, the space is calm, light and uncluttered.

The clothes come first, and Verdelilla describes its stock as unique pieces chosen by fashion professionals, and the labels are mostly independent designers from around the world rather than big fashion houses, with names like Apuntob, Barbara Tani, Giorgio Brato, Mother Denim, Absolut Cashmere and Injiri.

The accessories and smaller things — the jewellery, bags, shoes and lifestyle pieces — are chosen in the same spirit, and several of the brands work with recycled materials or artisanal production, so the emphasis is on things made with care rather than in vast quantities.

The shop sums up its approach as a matter of people, taste and choice, and says that its customers are what give meaning to those choices, which is a rather generous way for a shop to describe itself.

A boutique like this stands for a very different idea of shopping from the big chains on Via Roma, just a few streets away. It's slow, personal and selective, and in a city with a long tailoring tradition, that approach feels entirely at home.

[beat]

From here, walk a few minutes east to Porta Nuova station, then turn north up Via Roma, Turin's main arcaded shopping street, which leads all the way to Piazza San Carlo and on to Piazza Castello, past one shop window after another.
