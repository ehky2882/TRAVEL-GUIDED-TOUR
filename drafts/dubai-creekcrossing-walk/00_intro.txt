DUBAI W1 — THE CREEK CROSSING — 00 INTRO (walk)

This walk costs about a dirham and ends at gold.

You should be in Bur Dubai, at the entrance to the Textile Souk — the long wooden arcade set a few steps back from the water, close to the abra station. Over the next hour or so, you'll walk the length of the arcade, cross Dubai Creek on a wooden boat, and land on the Deira side, where the Spice Souk and the Gold Souk sit almost door to door.

Here is the shape of the thing. This route climbs the value chain of the old economy one commodity at a time: cloth on this bank, then the crossing itself, then spice, then gold — in roughly ascending order of what a kilogram is worth. Traders have run this exact circuit for well over a century, in both directions, in every season. You are simply running it for pleasure instead of profit.

A few honest notes before you start. The souks at either end are covered, so you'll be in shade for most of the walking. The crossing in the middle is open water with a breeze on it, and it lasts around five minutes. The total distance on foot is under two kilometres, and the lanes on the Deira side are narrow and busy — which is not a warning, it's the point.

Start inside the Textile Souk, straight ahead. We'll work our way through the arcade to the water.
