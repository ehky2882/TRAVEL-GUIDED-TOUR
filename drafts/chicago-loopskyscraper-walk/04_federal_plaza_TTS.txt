American fire codes required a building's structural steel to be buried in concrete or plaster, out of sight. mees wanted the frame visible, because to him the frame was the building's one honest fact. So he buried the real steel where the code insisted and bolted more steel to the outside in a perfect rhythm, whether or not a column stood behind it.

Eighty-nine years after jen ee, the frame has stopped being a solution to a problem and become the thing the building is about. Not hidden, not decorated. Advertised.
