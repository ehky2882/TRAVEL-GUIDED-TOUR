# Couleur Locale

**Atlas · Athens · 13 · Drink** — single-stop tour  
**Address:** Normanou 3, Monastiraki  
**Pin (WGS-84):** 37.976578, 23.724416  
**Length:** 286 words, about 1:54 spoken

---

The best view in Monastiraki is behind an antique shop, at the top of an old lift.

Find Normanou Street, a narrow side alley off Ermou, right in the middle of Monastiraki. Number three looks like nothing much from the street: an arcade, and an entrance through what appears to be a shop full of old furniture. Go in anyway. That's the point.

Past the furniture, you'll find a slightly old school lift. Take it up. The doors open onto a rooftop bar spread over the upper floors of the building, and straight ahead, filling the sky, is the Acropolis, with the Parthenon on top.

That's the whole gift, really. You've walked in off one of the busiest shopping streets in Athens, through a junk shop, up in a creaking lift, and come out at eye level with the most famous building in the western world. Athens does this better than almost anywhere.

As for the drinks: cocktails, wine, and plates to share. The crowd is mostly young and mostly local, and on a Friday or Saturday evening it's worth booking ahead for sunset.

*[beat]*

And here's what I like about it. Look across the rooftops from up here and you'll see dozens of other flat roofs, with laundry, plants, water tanks and chairs. The roof terrace is how ordinary Athenians have always escaped the street. Couleur Locale just put a bar on one and turned the lights on.

Before you go, take a walk up the rest of Normanou. The famous Poet Sandal Maker used to have his workshop on this very alley, before moving near the Acropolis Museum. And the flea market of Monastiraki is only a couple of minutes away, if you fancy rummaging.
