# Lycabettus Funicular

**Atlas · Athens · 30 · Observe** — single-stop tour  
**Address:** Aristippou 1 (at Ploutarchou), Kolonaki  
**Pin (WGS-84):** 37.980316, 23.744006  
**Length:** 328 words, about 2:11 spoken

---

The quickest way up the highest hill in central Athens is through the inside of it.

You should be at the top of Ploutarchou Street in Kolonaki, where it meets Aristippou, at the lower station of the Lycabettus funicular. The streets here are steep, the buildings stacked up the slope, and the station is where the city simply stops and the hill begins.

The funicular has been running since April nineteen sixty five. It's two hundred and ten metres long, and here's the gift detail: it runs entirely in a tunnel. There's no view on the way up, none at all. Then, about three minutes later, the doors open and you step out near the top of a two hundred and seventy seven metre hill, the highest point in central Athens.

At the summit there's the little white chapel of Saint George, from the nineteenth century, and a café and restaurant. Just below it is the open air theatre, which closed for fifteen years and reopened in twenty twenty three. Leonard Cohen, James Brown and Radiohead have all played there.

There's an old story about this hill. The goddess Athena was carrying a mountain of limestone to build up the Acropolis, received some bad news on the way, and dropped it. This, they say, is where it landed.

*[beat]*

Here's the reason to come, though. From the top you can see what Athens really is. Not the temples. The city: white apartment blocks running in every direction, all the way to the mountains on three sides and the sea on the fourth. Every one of them is a polykatoikia, and together they're the great unsung architecture of the twentieth century city. The Acropolis is the jewel. This is the setting.

If you've got the legs for it, skip the funicular on the way back and take the footpath down through the pines. It brings you out at the top of Kolonaki, a short walk from a well-earned coffee.
