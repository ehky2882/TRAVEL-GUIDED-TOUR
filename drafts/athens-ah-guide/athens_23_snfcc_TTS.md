The architect is Renzo Piano. He imagined the building rising from the ground as if a piece of the earth's crust had been lifted up. So he made an artificial hill and put the buildings inside it. The National Library is under the lower part of the slope, the Greek National Opera under the higher part, and the park runs right over the top of both, planted with native Greek plants.

Have a look at that canopy. Piano called it a cloud hovering over the hill. It's a huge, flat roof of concrete, covering roughly a hectare, sitting on thirty very slender steel columns, and it's covered in solar panels that help power the buildings beneath.
