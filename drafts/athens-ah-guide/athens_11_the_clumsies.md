# The Clumsies

**Atlas · Athens · 11 · Drink** — single-stop tour  
**Address:** Praxitelous 30  
**Pin (WGS-84):** 37.978972, 23.729831  
**Length:** 335 words, about 2:14 spoken

---

A bar named after its own spilt drinks has been ranked among the best in the world. Athens has a sense of humour.

You should be on Praxitelous Street, a busy, slightly scruffy road in the commercial centre, outside number thirty. Look for a restored old building with big windows and a café-bar feel on the ground floor, and people sitting there at almost any hour.

Start with the name, because the bar explains it itself. Many whoops. Droplets outside the shaker. Spatters on clothes and surfaces. It's a joke at their own expense, and it tells you a great deal about the place. The founders were two bartenders, Nikos Bakoulis and Vassilis Kyritsis, who had each won Greece's top bartending competition, in twenty eleven and twenty twelve, and who ended up as strangers behind the same bar. They found they shared a sense of self mockery, and a belief that a bartender is judged as much by how they talk to you as by the drink.

It's an all day bar. In the morning it's light and quiet, a place for coffee. As the day goes on, the mood changes, and by night it's full.

Now the gift detail. Up the inside stair is a private space they call the Room: a fireplace, a bookcase, a billiard table and a stack of records, for up to ten people, with a bartender of their own. Book ahead.

The Clumsies first made the World's Fifty Best Bars list in twenty fifteen, and in twenty twenty it was ranked third in the world.

*[beat]*

And here's the thing. A bar that good could easily have become solemn. This one decided early that it wouldn't, and that friendliness was the point. That's a very Athenian choice.

If you're making an evening of it, Baba au Rum is a couple of minutes' walk south, on Klitiou Street. It's been on the same world list for more than a decade. Two of the city's best bars, barely a street apart.
