# National Museum of Contemporary Art (EMST)

**Atlas · Athens · 24 · Visit** — single-stop tour  
**Address:** Leof. Kallirrois & Amvrosiou Frantzi  
**Pin (WGS-84):** 37.963016, 23.725262  
**Length:** 326 words, about 2:10 spoken

---

Athenians of a certain age still call this building by the name of a beer.

You should be on Syngrou Avenue, the wide road running down towards the sea, where it meets Kallirrois Avenue, looking at a long, low, pale building with its windows running in unbroken horizontal bands. This is the Fix building, now Greece's National Museum of Contemporary Art, known as EMST.

Fix was a brewery, one of the great names in Greek beer, and it moved to this spot in eighteen ninety three, on the bank of the Ilissos river, which today runs hidden underground. In nineteen fifty seven the Fix family asked the architect Takis Zenetos to redesign the whole factory. He finished in nineteen sixty one.

Have a look at those long bands of glass. That's Zenetos. He stretched the building out along the avenue, so that from a passing car it reads as one long, clean, fast line. It was the age of the car arriving in Athens, and this was its architecture: modern, confident, and built to be seen at speed.

Then things went wrong. The brewery was abandoned in the late nineteen seventies, and in the mid nineteen nineties, after the site was taken over for the new metro, the northern part of the building was demolished. What survived was handed to the new museum. After an architectural competition and a long conversion, EMST finally opened its doors to the public in twenty twenty.

*[beat]*

And here's the thing. Greece's museum of contemporary art lives inside a factory that was itself one of the boldest modern buildings in the country. The building is part of the collection. Look at it the way you'd look at anything on the walls inside.

From here, the Acropolis Museum is a ten minute walk north, and the contrast is worth making: two of the city's newest museums, one looking back two and a half thousand years, the other only as far as yesterday.
