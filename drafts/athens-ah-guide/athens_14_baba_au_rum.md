# Baba au Rum

**Atlas · Athens · 14 · Drink** — single-stop tour  
**Address:** Klitiou 6  
**Pin (WGS-84):** 37.977399, 23.729783  
**Length:** 293 words, about 1:57 spoken

Behind this bar there are more than four hundred rums. You could visit every night for a year and never drink the same one twice.

You should be on Klitiou Street, a narrow lane in the city centre, outside a small bar with a modernist look and the name Baba au Rum over the door. Like the cake, yes. Soaked in rum. That's very much the joke.

The bar calls itself a rum and cocktail society. It was started by the bartender Thanos Prunarus more than fifteen years ago, and it still runs under his eye. The style borrows from the old tiki tradition, the American dream of the South Seas in a glass, and then runs off somewhere of its own.

Ask for the house favourite, the Spicy Baba, which has been the most ordered drink here since twenty ten. Or try something from the current list, like the Supremus number fifty eight, their twist on the Caribbean Ti' Punch, made with a blend of rums, falernum, tea and summer fruit.

Now the gift detail. In twenty twenty five, Baba au Rum was on the World's Fifty Best Bars list for the eleventh year running, and that same year the list gave it a special award for staying power, the Legend of the List.

*[beat]*

Here's what that means, standing on this little street. Eleven years in the world's top fifty is extremely hard to do, and it's been done, quietly, by a small bar in a side lane of Athens. Most cities would make a monument of it. Athens just keeps the lights on.

From here, walk two minutes north to Praxitelous Street. The Clumsies, another of the city's world-ranked bars, is at number thirty. Same evening, two very different moods.
