Pasaje 94

For twenty-five years, the door to this ground-floor space stayed firmly shut, and the city more or less forgot what lay behind it.

A short walk from the river gardens, on the quiet Carrer dels Trinitaris in the northern part of the old town, the doorway at number thirteen opens into a single room with several jobs to do.

Before its revival this was a storeroom, then a garage, and then nothing at all. In 2025 a team of architects took it on and opened it as Pasaje 94, and their approach was to recover and keep as much as they could of what was already there rather than strip it back, so the old structure and surfaces remain part of the room.

It does three things at once, as an architecture studio, where the team works, a showroom for carefully chosen design objects, and a specialty coffee bar, which is where most visitors come in, and the layout is deliberately open and flexible, so the three jobs blend into one another through the day.

The coffee has built a following of its own, and the café has been listed among the best places for a carefully made cup in the city. It keeps fairly early hours, opening in the morning and closing in the early evening, and it's closed on Sundays and Mondays.

The real story here is the street itself. The old town is full of ground-floor units like this one, shuttered and gathering dust for decades, and the founders describe this one as abandoned and forgotten by the rest of the city, which makes their small recovery feel like a model for what could be done with many others.

Take your coffee to go and walk north to the Turia gardens, or wander back through the surrounding streets and keep an eye out for other old shopfronts and doorways quietly waiting for their own second life.
