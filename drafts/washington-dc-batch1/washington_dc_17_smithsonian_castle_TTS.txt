Here is what the Castle is for, on a Mall where everything else is federal. The Smithsonian is a private bequest, run by a board that includes the Chief Justice and the Vice President, housed on federal land, funded mostly by Congress, and not quite a government agency. The red building is the seam made visible: it belongs to the city of museums and to the capital at the same time.
