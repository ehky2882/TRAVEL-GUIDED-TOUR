# Mumbai 07 · Eau Bar, The Oberoi

*Vantage: on the Marine Drive promenade at Nariman Point, facing The Oberoi* · 333 words · about 133 s

The best seat for a Bombay sunset is a matter of fierce local opinion. This is one serious contender.

You're at the southern tip of Marine Drive, at Nariman Point, on the promenade by the sea wall. Turn so the water is behind you and look for the tall hotel marked The Oberoi. The Eau Bar is inside it, with an open terrace that looks straight out over the Arabian Sea — the same water you've just had your back to.

Have a look along the curve of the bay first, to your right as you face the hotel. That long crescent of road and lights is Marine Drive, and from up on the terrace at dusk it turns into what everyone here calls the Queen's Necklace — the streetlamps coming on one by one round the edge of the bay, like a string of pearls.

Now the room itself. The Oberoi describes the Eau Bar's interiors as Art Deco, with red velvet and a modern take on the Tree of Life motif — a nod to the Art Deco apartment blocks that line Marine Drive, which you can see from the terrace. The cocktail list was put together by the British bartender Andrew Pearson. It opens at five in the evening and runs until half past one in the morning, with a DJ at weekends, so sunset and the late crowd overlap rather neatly.

What's worth knowing, standing here, is what this hotel has been through. In November two thousand and eight, The Oberoi was one of the targets of the attacks on Mumbai, and guests and staff were killed inside. The hotel closed, was rebuilt, and reopened in twenty ten. Coming here for a drink and the view is, in its quiet way, part of the city's answer to that.

[beat]

If you're not going up, the promenade is a perfectly good substitute. Walk north along the sea wall as the light goes, and the necklace will switch itself on round you.
