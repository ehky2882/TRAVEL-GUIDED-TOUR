# Mumbai 12 · Bastian At The Top

*Vantage: on N C Kelkar Marg, Dadar West, at the foot of Kohinoor Square* · 310 words · about 124 s

Forty-eight floors up, there's a restaurant designed to feel like a cave. It's a surprisingly good joke.

You're on N C Kelkar Marg in Dadar West, at the foot of Kohinoor Square, one of the tallest towers in this part of the city. Look up: Bastian At The Top occupies the forty-eighth floor.

Bastian is one of the best-known restaurant names in Mumbai, co-owned by the restaurateur Ranjit Bindra and the actor Shilpa Shetty. The original opened in Bandra in twenty sixteen, and this rooftop version opened in twenty twenty-three. It's big — four hundred and fifty seats — and divided between a main bar called the Forest and a quieter dining room. Bindra and the designer Minal Chopra went for earthy tones and curved, cave-like surfaces, with a slightly mythological air. There's a swimming pool, open at weekends.

Have a look at the menu if you're going up. It began as a seafood restaurant, and the seafood is still the draw — steamed garlic prawns, the much-photographed lobster bomb — alongside burrata, Mongolian lamb and rice bowls at lunch. The guide that sent you here calls it Mumbai's largest bar; that's a claim I'd leave to the owners, but nobody would call it small.

And here's the thing about Dadar. This is not glamour country. It's one of the most Marathi parts of Mumbai, a neighbourhood of flower markets, cricket and political rallies at Shivaji Park, a few hundred metres away. Putting the city's most celebrity-heavy dining room on top of a tower here says a lot about where Mumbai's money is moving: north from the old Fort, into the middle of the city.

If you've time before going up, walk to Shivaji Park. It's where Sachin Tendulkar learned his cricket, and on a weekend evening there will be dozens of matches running at once on the dusty ground.
