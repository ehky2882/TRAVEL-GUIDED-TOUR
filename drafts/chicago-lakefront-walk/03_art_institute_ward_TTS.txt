ATLAS — CHICAGO
Walk 3: The Lakefront — Millennium Park to Museum Campus
Segment 03 — The Art Institute, east face, from the park
TTS-SAFE version

---

It's eighteen ninety-two, and the most unpopular man in Chicago decides not to sue.

You should be on the park side of the Art Institute, south of Millennium Park, with the museum's east flank in front of you and the footbridge running over Monroe toward it. From here you can see the two things this whole walk turns on at the same time: a large museum, and a large amount of empty park.

The empty part is the achievement. Aaron Montgomery Ward spent twenty years and four trips to the Illinois Supreme Court keeping buildings out of this park, and he was loathed for it — a rich man in the newspapers, blocking things ordinary people wanted, at his own expense. The park around you is the result. The argument was settled over a century ago and doesn't need relitigating here.

What's worth standing here for is the exception.

Ward filed his first complaint in eighteen ninety. Two years later, when construction started on the Art Institute, he said nothing. No injunction, no protest, and by most accounts only grudging assent — and it happened because the World's Columbian Exposition needed a hall for its lectures nearer downtown than Jackson Park, and the Art Institute needed a permanent home, so one building was made to solve both problems and the museum moved in after the fair went home.

He wasn't perfectly consistent, either. A few years later he agreed to a temporary post office in the park, at a moment when his own mail-order company was the largest single customer the United States Postal Service had.

And because nobody ever challenged it, this is the one building here that has been allowed to grow. It has expanded again and again for a hundred and thirty years, right where a court would later rule that nothing could be built at all. One decision not to file a piece of paper, and the museum in front of you is the only structure in Chicago's front yard that gets to keep getting bigger.

Keep going south through the park. You'll see the fountain before you reach it.
