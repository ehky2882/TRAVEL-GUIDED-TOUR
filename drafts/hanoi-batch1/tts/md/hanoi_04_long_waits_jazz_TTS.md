# Long Waits Jazz

To get a piano up these stairs, they had to take the stairs apart.

You're in the alley at number five nwen kwang bick, on the western edge of the Old Quarter. Long Waits is upstairs, the whole first floor of an old house, wedged between a café and somebody's home. If you can see a staircase in dark, worn looking wood heading up from the alley, that's your way in. It looks old. It's actually brand new; they built it to suit the house.

The club was started in twenty twenty three by the saxophonist nwen bow long and two friends. bow long has been a fixture of Vietnamese jazz for years, and the club is his bandstand. One of the friends had learned saxophone from him; the other oversaw the renovation, which included that piano. To hoist it in they removed the banisters, rigged a lifting system, and put everything back afterwards.

Upstairs, the floor is one room: a stage, a bar, and just enough space for a small audience. The walls and stage are painted a warm maroon brown. The ceiling and the bar are left bare, so the old house still shows through. Light comes from the stage and from one glass cube hanging over the bar, and that's more or less it.

Have a look at the programme on the way in. The band has been working through a hundred classic jazz albums, one a week, start to finish. There's a Sonny Rollins night. There's a Bass Night, when eight bass players take turns.

And here's what I find rather lovely about it. This is a listening room. Not a bar with music in the corner, a place where people actually stop talking. In a city that runs on noise, someone climbed a staircase, hauled up a piano, and made a room for quiet attention. bow long uses it to give young players a stage, picking them out at student recitals.

Come back after dark; the music starts in the evening and runs to around midnight. And if you've a little time beforehand, walk ten minutes west to the Hanoi Flag Tower, built in eighteen twelve, and have a look at the city's oldest lookout before you go and sit in its newest listening room.
