The hotel closed in nineteen eighty-one and came back in the nineties with a tower added onto the back. Stand at the curb and you can hold both halves at once: a lobby where a reporter nobody had heard of handed over a manuscript, and a ballroom where, four years later, the party thrown for it had a guest list the city enforced.
