## 🔴 Three stops depict copyrighted artwork — owner cleared them to ship (2026-08-17)

The US has **no freedom of panorama for artworks** (17 USC §120 exempts *architectural works*
only), so photographing these creates a derivative work. Owner direction was "keep going on
all 3", i.e. the Pilsen precedent: **ship, and log the obligation as OPEN rather than cleared.**

| # | work | artist | how exposed |
|---|------|--------|-------------|
| 04 | *Atlanta from the Ashes* (the phoenix) | 1969 | **possibly PD** — pre-1978, notice was required and public sculpture often lacked it. Commons hosts 5 photos, one CC0. Suggestive, not proof |
| 06 | *Behold* | Patrick Morelli, **1990** | **unambiguously in copyright** — post-1978, automatic, no notice needed |
| 17 | Krog Street Tunnel murals | many, repainted continuously | in copyright, and **unavoidable** — a photo without the art is just an underpass |

**Krog has no rightsholder to approach** even if you wanted to clear it — the walls are
repainted constantly by many hands. That is different from Pilsen, where Valadez is living.

**06 has a clean escape if you ever want one:** lead with Ebenezer's 1922 Heritage Sanctuary,
a building, and let the narration carry the statue. The script opens by saying people misread
the statue anyway.

**Trap for future sessions: an owner-supplied photo does NOT clear this.** It clears the
photographer, never the artist.

## ⚠️ transcriptText — THREE header formats inside one city

Unlike previous cities, the format **alternates per file**. Detect it; never strip a fixed
number of lines.

| fmt | shape | scripts |
|-----|-------|---------|
| **A** | `Corpus:` / `Walk: none` / `Segment: NN of 45` / `Position:` then a `---` rule | 01, 02, 03, 04, 09, 22, 23, 24 |
| **B** | **no header at all** — opens on prose | 05–08, 10–13, 16–21, 25, 26, 27 |
| **C** | `ATLANTA — STOP NN` / TITLE / `Clean version`, **no `---` rule** | 14, 15, 28, 29, 30 |

**Rule that covers all three:** if line 1 starts with `Corpus:` take everything after the
first `---`; if it starts with `ATLANTA — STOP` drop the first three lines; otherwise take
the file whole. Beat markers are bare `[beat]`, no asterisks — one per script, all 30.

**`transcriptText` must come from the clean `.txt`, never the `_TTS.txt`.** The TTS files are
heavily phoneticised (`may ree ett uh`, `muhs koh gee`, `chat uh noo guh`, `eb uh nee zer`,
`pahnss duh lee on`) and would ship gibberish to a reader. They also carry **no header at
all**, in every format.

## ✅ CORRECTION (2026-08-24) — Commons is NOT blocked; the earlier read was wrong

**Everything in the post-mortem below about the rate limit is accurate as history, but its
conclusion — that Commons was unusable — was not.** The window reopens. A grabber that honours
`Retry-After`, spaces downloads ~4s, and backs off exponentially pulls **12+ files per subject**
without tripping anything. `/tmp/cgrab.py` in that session was the working shape:

- **The failure mode that fooled me twice:** when throttled, `list=categorymembers` returns an
  HTTP error, and a naive caller reports it as **"0 files"** — indistinguishable from an empty
  category. Two separate sessions concluded "Commons is dry for Atlanta" from exactly this.
  **Always distinguish transport failure from an empty result.** Probe a category that is known
  to hold files; if that also reads 0, you are throttled, not out of material.
- **`list=search` (namespace 6) throttles just as hard** but works with wide spacing, and is how
  you find a category name instead of guessing it. `Woodruff Park` → 793 hits.
- **I re-made the "five of my first guesses were wrong" mistake** — probed `Category:Atlanta
  BeltLine` (0) when the verified name `Category:BeltLine, Atlanta` was already written down
  below. **Read the verified list before probing.**

**Counts confirmed live 2026-08-24:** Krog Street Tunnel 59 · Spelman College 59 · Hurt Building
50 · Flatiron Building (Atlanta) 35 · Morehouse College 28 · Historic Fourth Ward Park 12 ·
Georgian Terrace Hotel 10 · The Temple (Atlanta) 6 · Prince Hall Masonic Temple (Atlanta) 3.

**Dedupe by content hash, not filename.** Repeated category listings re-serve the same files
under new labels — the Hurt pool read 17 and held 12. Do this *before* saving.

**Licensing note:** Commons for Atlanta is mostly CC BY / CC BY-SA (credit rows required in
`drafts/CREDITS.md`), but there is real CC0 in there — 4 of 9 Flatirons and 2 of 4 Temples.
**Prefer the CC0 file when it is good enough**; it costs nothing downstream.

## Sourcing — what happened, so the next session doesn't repeat it

**Stock (Unsplash / Pexels / Pixabay) returns the *city*, not the *place*.** 512 images
fetched; it worked for exactly two subjects — the Capitol, and Jackson Street Bridge, whose
whole point *is* the skyline view. Everything else came back as generic Atlanta skylines, or
worse: **Barcelona's Central Market** for Ponce City Market, a **Penang, Malaysia** mural for
Krog, **Utah's and Iowa's** state capitols for Georgia's, and **ten wrong theatres** — Oakland's
Fox, the LA Orpheum, TCL Chinese — for the Fox.

**Wikimedia Commons is where these subjects live, and it is currently rate-limiting us.**
Every category name below was verified to exist and hold files. Nothing here is a thin subject.

**🔴 The block was self-inflicted, and it is the reason this batch stalled.** Two full
30-subject passes were fired with **bare category names** (the pipeline needs the `Category:`
prefix; without it the API answers `invalidcategory` and the pipeline swallowed it as an empty
result), then a third correct pass, then repeated probes — several times a normal city's
request volume in about two hours, against a host whose burst limit is documented in
`CLAUDE.md` with ~1.5s pacing the pipeline never applied. Chicago, Berlin and Dubai never
tripped it because they never did that.

**All four pipeline defects are now fixed** in `drafts/pipeline/` — see that commit. Silent
`invalidcategory`, 429-as-bad-image, portrait-drop, and `crop43`'s centre-crop.

**Measured behaviour of the limit:** roughly 10 files land, then 429 regardless of
inter-request gap until the window resets. `/tmp/slowgrab.py` is resumable and time-boxed for
exactly this. **Before running it, probe once** — if `upload.wikimedia.org` returns 200 the
window is open; if 429, wait rather than retry, because retrying extends it.

**Routes that do NOT work around it:** Openverse indexes these images but serves them *from*
`upload.wikimedia.org` (100% of large Atlanta results). Flickr-via-Openverse is reachable but
caps at 759×487 without an API key, which needs a Pro account the owner declined.
