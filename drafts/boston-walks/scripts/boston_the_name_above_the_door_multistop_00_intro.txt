# BOSTON · W5 — The Name Above the Door
Introduction · walk · segment 1 of 6 · clean · 29 September 2026
---
This walk starts under one name and ends under another, and a congregation once walked it the other way.

You should be in Smith Court, a short lane off Joy Street on the north side of Beacon Hill, with the plain brick front of the African Meeting House set back along the lane. From here the walk goes down the hill to Faneuil Hall, a little over a mile with the stops. Joy Street drops steeply to Cambridge Street on brick sidewalks that are narrow and uneven, then the walk crosses at the light and goes over City Hall Plaza, which is wide, open and exposed to the weather, with heavy traffic along its edges. After that it is level to the market.

Two of the stops can be entered. The meeting house and the school beside it are a museum, open Monday through Saturday by ticket, with a ramp and an elevator into the meeting house. At the far end, the hall's upstairs room is free when the city isn't using it, and rangers give short talks inside. This walk keeps to the outside of both, so it works at any hour.

The subject is names. Every place on the way has one that was chosen, moved or removed, and the walk begins and ends under two that were put over doors on purpose. The first is in front of you, in Smith Court.
