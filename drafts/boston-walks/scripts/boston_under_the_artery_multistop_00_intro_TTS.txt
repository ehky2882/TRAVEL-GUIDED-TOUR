# BOSTON · W3 — Under the Artery
Under the Artery — Introduction · walk · segment 1 of 6 · TTS-safe · 29 September 2026
---
This walk follows one street. For the opening stretch, the street isn't there.

You're at the Congress Street edge of City Hall Plaza, City Hall's concrete on your left and the brick of the plaza opening ahead. Until the nineteen sixties, Hanover Street began about where you are and ran from here all the way to the water, through the middle of downtown and the length of the North End. Boston cut it in two places, at two times, for two reasons. One cut was never undone. The other was undone completely. This walk goes from the cut that was kept to the cut that healed, then onto the street that was never touched, and finishes on the hill above it.

Five places: the plaza where the street's downtown end disappeared, the market stalls that sold through both clearances, the Greenway where a missing block came back, Hanover Street itself, and kops Hill Burying Ground at the top of the neighborhood. You'll cross the red brick line of the Freedom Trail at a right angle and keep going.

The route is flat until the last segment, which climbs one steep block up Hull Street. On Fridays and Saturdays the market is crowded and Blackstone Street is slow going. The burying ground is open roughly nine to four; if you arrive late, the view from the Hull Street gate does most of the work.

Turn to face the plaza, and we'll start with the street that isn't there.
