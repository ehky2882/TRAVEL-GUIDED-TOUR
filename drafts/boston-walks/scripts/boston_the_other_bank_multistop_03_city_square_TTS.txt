# BOSTON · W4 — The Other Bank
City Square · walk · segment 4 of 5 · TTS-safe · 29 September 2026
---
There is a floor plan in the grass here, and nothing built on it.

You're in City Square Park, at the foot of the hill. Set into the lawn is a low outline of brick and stone, a square with a few inner lines: the footprint of a building gone before the Revolution.

In sixteen twenty nine the colony's advance party built a timber hall here and called it the Great House. When John Winthrop arrived the next summer with the main body of settlers he moved into it, and the colony's government met here, on this bank, for about three months. Then the water failed, sickness spread through the camp, and Winthrop and most of the company crossed to the peninsula opposite, where a spring had been found, and named the new town Boston.

The Great House became a meeting hall and then, from sixteen thirty five, a tavern, kept by one family until the town burned in seventeen seventy five. Archaeologists found the foundations in the nineteen eighties, as the highway that once ran over this square came down: a fieldstone cellar about twelve feet on a side, paved with brick, and under it the postholes of something older. The outline follows what they found.

The colony began here and left within the year. Boston's story starts in a building Boston never had.

Walk toward the water onto the Charlestown Bridge and take the harbor side sidewalk, on your left. Stop at the benches near the middle and look across at the North End.
