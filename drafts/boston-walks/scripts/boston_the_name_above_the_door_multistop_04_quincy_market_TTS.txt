# BOSTON · W5 — The Name Above the Door
Quincy Market · walk · segment 5 of 6 · TTS-safe · 29 September 2026
---
The name carved over this building is not the name people use for it.

You're between the back of fan yul Hall and the front of kwin zee Market, at the columned end of the long granite hall, looking down its length toward the harbor. The market is granite, and the hall at your back is brick. Over the columns the building announces itself as kwin zee Market, for the mayor who pushed it through in the eighteen twenties, and for most of its life that was what the city called it. Then in nineteen seventy six the whole complex was restored and reopened, and the name it was given for the reopening was the hall's. The name fan yul Hall Marketplace covers this building and the two long rows beside it, and when people say they're meeting at fan yul Hall, this is nearly always where they mean.

The name spread. It left the small building and settled over the large ones, so a merchant who never saw this market has his name on it anyway, and the mayor who built it keeps his over one door. Names in this city behave that way. They don't stay attached to the thing they were given to. They move to whatever is larger, and they outlast the argument about whether they should.

Turn around now and face the brick hall. The doors in the middle of its front, the ones facing you, are the way into the upstairs room, and they are the last stop.
