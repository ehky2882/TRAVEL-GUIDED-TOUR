# BOSTON · W1 — Downhill
00 · Introduction · walk · segment 1 of 6 · TTS-safe · 29 September 2026
---
This walk goes downhill once, and then it never goes up again.

You're on Boston Common, on the path that runs along its upper edge beside Beacon Street, with the State House dome across the traffic in front of you. This is the top of the route. From here it runs west for about a mile and a half: down Beacon Street to Charles Street, across into the Public Garden, along the middle of Commonwealth Avenue, and left to kop lee Square. The descent is real for the first third of a mile. After Charles Street the ground is level, and it stays level to the end.

Boston is a city that moved its own earth, and on this route you can feel how far each piece of it traveled. The hill under you gave up its top to a pond a few hundred yards north. The street at the bottom of the slope was carried down from a knoll a few hundred feet above it. And the ground you'll end on came from another town entirely, and arrived on a timetable. Every step west lands on earth that traveled a little farther to get there.

One practical note. The one real intersection is Charles Street at Beacon, at the foot of the hill. Past that, the route is level enough for anyone.

Turn to face the State House. It's the first stop, and the highest.
