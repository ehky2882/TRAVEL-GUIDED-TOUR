# BOSTON · W4 — The Other Bank
The Other Bank — Introduction · walk · segment 1 of 5 · TTS-safe · 29 September 2026
---
This walk starts on the side of the river that Boston's stories were aimed at, and it ends looking back.

You're on Pier One in the Charlestown Navy Yard, outside the fence that screens visitors to the ship, with the black hull and three masts of USS Constitution in front of you. Most people arrive in Charlestown at the end of a long day, following the red brick line of the Freedom Trail to its finish. This walk begins where that line stops and goes the other way, back toward the water and across it.

Four places. The ship, which Boston built and Charlestown has kept. Bunker Hill, the battle Boston watched from its rooftops. City Square, where the colony's government met before there was a Boston to move it to. And the Charlestown Bridge, halfway across the river, facing the steeple whose lanterns were hung to be read from this bank.

A few practical notes. Boarding the ship takes a photo ID and a security check, her hours change through the year, and she closes on the days she sails, so this walk reads her from the pier. From the yard it is a steady climb of ten minutes to Monument Square, then downhill to City Square, then a long bridge with wind on it. The monument's stairs sometimes close for weather; the walk doesn't depend on them.

Face the ship, and we'll begin with the one thing here that came across the water on purpose.
