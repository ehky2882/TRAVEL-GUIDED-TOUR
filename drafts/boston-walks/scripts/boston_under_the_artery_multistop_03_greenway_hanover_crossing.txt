# BOSTON · W3 — Under the Artery
The Greenway at Hanover Street · walk · segment 4 of 6 · clean · 29 September 2026
---
The block you're about to cross did not exist for fifty years.

You're on the Greenway where Hanover Street crosses it, the market behind you, the North End Parks ahead, their long pergola to one side and the lawn opening toward the neighborhood.

The block between Blackstone Street and Cross Street was demolished in the nineteen fifties for the Central Artery, the elevated highway that carried through traffic over this line. For half a century Hanover Street ended at a highway pier here and started again on the other side. The block came back in 2004, when the last of the deck was taken down and the traffic went into the tunnel beneath this lawn. The parks opened in 2008. Hanover Street now runs from the North End onto the Greenway and from the Greenway into the market.

*[beat]*

You've walked past both cuts now. At the plaza, the city removed the street's downtown end and left the ground open; sixty years later it drew the old line back as a ramp with the old name. Here it removed one block, kept the traffic, and then found a way to have both: the cars underneath, the block on top. One cut is a memory with a name on it. This one is a street again.

Hanover Street runs uphill from here. Follow it a few blocks to Prince Street, to the brick church on your left, and turn around.
