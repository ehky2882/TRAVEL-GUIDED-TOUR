# BOSTON · W3 — Under the Artery
Hanover Street · walk · segment 5 of 6 · TTS-safe · 29 September 2026
---
Every street you've walked on since the plaza was moved, cut or rebuilt. This one held its place.

You're at Hanover and Prince Streets, beside St. Leonard's, facing back down Hanover toward the Greenway, the North End behind you and downtown ahead. From here the street runs straight to the lawn, and past the lawn the towers of downtown sit where its far end used to be.

Hanover is the North End's main street, and it has been since the seventeen hundreds, when it carried the town's road out to the North End's wharves and the ferry to Charlestown. St. Leonard's, at your shoulder, was founded as a parish in eighteen seventy three by Italian immigrants. The point here is the street's direction.

Beacon Hill kept two Bostons a few blocks apart. The Back Bay set a new one down on a plan. Chinatown's was carried here from another continent. The North End's version of the pattern is different again: it is the neighborhood the city kept building its way around. The highway drew a line along its edge and left it on the far side. The plaza took the far end of its main street. The interstate went under it. Through all of that, Hanover Street pointed at downtown, and the neighborhood behind it kept its shape.

Look down the street at where the plaza is. Nothing about the view tells you the street once got there. The gap at the bottom of the hill is the one you crossed at the Greenway. The gap you can't see is the one at the top of the plaza.

Turn around and walk up Hanover past the church. The last stop is uphill: turn left on Prince Street, right on Salem Street, then left up Hull Street past Old North to the gate of kops Hill Burying Ground. Go in and walk through to the far corner, above Snowhill Street.
