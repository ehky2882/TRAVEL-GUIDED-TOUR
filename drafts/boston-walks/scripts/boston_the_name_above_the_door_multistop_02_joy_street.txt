# BOSTON · W5 — The Name Above the Door
Joy Street to Cambridge Street · walk · segment 3 of 6 · clean · 29 September 2026
---
The street you're about to walk down used to be called Belknap Street.

You're at the top of Joy Street where Smith Court meets it, looking down the slope to Cambridge Street. The families who built and used the meeting house lived along this slope, on Belknap Street and the lanes off it, and their addresses have changed twice since: the street was renamed in the nineteenth century, and the houses were renumbered with it. A house that stood at one number on Belknap stands at another on Joy. The buildings didn't move. The names did.

Start down. Partway along, Phillips Street crosses on your left, with Lewis Hayden's house along it, a stop on another of these walks. Keep straight.

*[beat]*

At the bottom, Cambridge Street is wide and busy, and the neighborhood across it is the West End. When the meeting house was new, the slope you've just walked was counted as the West End too; the name reached up the hill. In the late 1950s the city took the old West End down, almost to this street, for new housing. The name survived the buildings. The signs still say West End, over towers and a hospital.

Cross Cambridge Street at the light with City Hall's concrete bulk ahead of you. The open ground in front of it is City Hall Plaza, and the next segment is the crossing.
