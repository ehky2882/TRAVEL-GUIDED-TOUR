# MIAMI W1 · 07 — The Argument, Still Running
W1 · The Pitch · segment 7 · S1 · TTS-safe · 24 September 2026

---

Go past the torch and all the way through the park to the promenade along the water, and face east, out across the bay.

On the island directly across from you is the port, one of the busiest cruise ports, and if a ship is in when you arrive, you're looking at a floating resort several city blocks long. Its passengers board on that island, and when they sail, they slide past this park with the whole skyline you've just walked through lined up along the rail. For a great many of them it is the first sight of the city and the last.

Now turn around and face the boulevard. Every stop on this walk is somewhere behind that wall of buildings: the cottage by the river, the courthouse tower, the theater, the church, the newspaper's crown. Each was built to make a case to someone, and each case needed an audience that could be reached. In eighteen ninety seven the audience came down on the railroad and was reached with a hotel. In nineteen twenty five it was reached with five hundred and eight pages of newsprint. The towers along this boulevard now are marketed floor by floor to buyers who may live anywhere on earth, and the city's case now rests largely on the sight of itself from a ship's deck.

The founding story of this place is usually told as a single deal, struck by letter in eighteen ninety five. It reads better as the opening line of a sales pitch that never stopped, made first to a few hundred winter guests and now to whole shiploads at a time, with the skyline serving as the brochure.

When you're ready to leave, the Metromover has a station on the boulevard side of the park, and the loop will carry you back past half of what you've just walked, a story up and out of the sun.
