# MIAMI W4 · 00 — Intro — the west end of Charles Avenue

W4 · The Grove · segment 00 · plants the commute and the stone · TTS-safe · 24 September 2026

---

This street exists because the town said no. In the late eighteen hundreds the Bahamian families settled along it asked Coconut Grove for a road, and when the town refused they quarried the local limestone and laid one themselves. The state marker at the far end tells it that way, and this walk follows the road they built.

You're at the west end of Charles Avenue near Douglas Road, beside the Charlotte Jane Memorial Park Cemetery gate, a little under a mile south of the Douglas Road Metrorail station. The route runs east along Charles Avenue for about half a mile to Main Highway, jogs a quarter mile south to a stone church, comes back north past the Barnacle to Peacock Park, and then follows South Bayshore Drive for roughly two and a half miles to the gates of viz kye uh and the Metrorail station beside them. The first legs are easy sidewalk walking; the last is long and open, walked or ridden.

The shape matters, because this is a commute. For a generation people left these blocks each morning for the jobs along the bay and walked home at night to a neighborhood they had built for themselves, road included. Every stop ahead is somewhere a person from this street went to work.

Keep an eye on the stone. It made this road's first surface, it will be in the church's walls, and it returns at the end. Walk east; the first stop is the avenue itself.
