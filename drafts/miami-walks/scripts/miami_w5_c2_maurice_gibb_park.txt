# MIAMI W5 · C2 — Maurice Gibb Memorial Park

W5 · The Water Line · segment C2 · S2 factual · walk connective · clean · 24 September 2026

---

You are in Maurice Gibb Memorial Park, at Eighteenth Street and Purdy Avenue, named for the member of the Bee Gees who made his home on the Beach. Walk out to one of the lookouts and face west, across the bay toward the downtown skyline.

Part of this ground was once a filling station, and when the city rebuilt the park, reopening it in April of 2025, crews first had to clean the soil the station had left behind. Along the edge they planted mangroves, the salt-tolerant trees that lined this shore before the dredges and the fill replaced them, so the park now grows back, on made ground, the very plant that making it removed.

From here the bay looks like the reason for everything. The water in front of you is what sold the lots behind you, and nearly every balcony on this side of the island is turned to face it. Before you leave, look down where the lawn meets the water: in the same rebuild, that edge was raised, so the shore under your feet now stands above the line of the old one.

Count it with the causeway's rise. Then leave the park on its north side and walk up Purdy Avenue, past the boat slips and the low warehouse buildings, toward Twentieth Street and the center of Sunset Harbour.
