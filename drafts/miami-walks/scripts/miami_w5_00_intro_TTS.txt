# MIAMI W5 · 00 — Intro, the Lenox mouth

W5 · The Water Line · segment 00 · walk intro · plants the step · TTS-safe · 24 September 2026

---

Look at the paving first. The mall's patterned stone runs east from here toward the ocean for about half a mile, and at Lenox Avenue it simply stops, with a curb and a strip of plain asphalt where the cars take over again. This walk is about a handful of edges like that one, some a few inches high and one a good deal more, and about what each of them tells you regarding the ground underneath.

The route covers about a mile, flat and on sidewalks the whole way, along the bay side of Miami Beach: one block west to Alton Road, then north and west to the foot of the Venetian Causeway, into a small park on the water, and up Purdy Avenue into Sunset Harbour, a neighborhood of old warehouses and newer apartment buildings where the walk ends. The city rebuilds this district in stages, so if fencing blocks a corner when you arrive, use the marked crossing nearest to it.

Nothing along the way is old by the standards of most cities, and much of the ground itself was made by people rather than found. Keep a rough tally of every step up or down as you go, and notice which way the steps trend.

Walk one block west, to the corner of Alton Road.
