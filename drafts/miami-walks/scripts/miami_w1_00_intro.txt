# MIAMI W1 · 00 — The Pitch: Introduction
W1 · The Pitch · segment 0 · S1 + S6 · clean · 24 September 2026

---

Start with the track over your head. The small driverless cars of the Metromover run on it in loops through downtown, and for this walk they are your way in, your way out, and your shortcut whenever the heat wins; a station stands beside this first stop and another beside the last.

The route runs a little under two miles on flat ground, from the river to the bay by way of the old courthouse, Flagler Street and Biscayne Boulevard, and takes about an hour and a half with the stops. Be honest about the sun: from late morning on, for most of the year, it is serious here, the boulevard offers very little shade, and water is not optional. Early morning and the last hours of daylight are kinder, and a fenced block or two of roadwork won't affect any stop.

What ties the stops together is persuasion. Every building on this route went up to convince somebody of something: winter visitors that a frontier town was a resort, a county that it would last, families that a boomtown was worth putting down roots in, moviegoers that the sky could be improved on, and readers that the land was worth buying. Treat each one as a sales document in stone and stucco, and ask who it was trying to reach.

The first stop is a few steps from here, beside the river: a small wooden house that outlived the hotel it was built to serve.
