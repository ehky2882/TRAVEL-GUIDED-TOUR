# MIAMI W2 · 00 — Intro: South Pointe Park entrance
W2 · Ocean Drive · segment 00 · S3 · walk-level facet: the losses wrote the law · TTS-safe · 24 September 2026

---

This walk runs north, and the story it tells runs the other way: each stop you reach turns out to be an earlier chapter than the one before it, until the last block of Lincoln Road hands you the afternoon where the whole thing began.

The route starts at the entrance to South Pointe Park, at the southern tip of Miami Beach, and covers about two miles of flat ground: along the Art Deco row on Ocean Drive, a block inland to Collins Avenue, then through Española Way to Lincoln Road. Shade is generous in the park and scarce after it, so in the warm months carry water and walk early or late.

Ocean Drive itself has been rearranged several times since twenty twenty, closed to cars, reopened to one lane, fitted with bike lanes and argued over in court, and may be laid out differently the day you walk it. None of that changes the route, since everything this walk asks you to see can be seen from the sidewalk.

The subject is the row of low hotels you are about to meet, and a question that sounds simpler than it is: why are they still standing? The honest answer involves three buildings you will not see, because each of them came down, and each one taught the city something.

The first stop is at the far end of the park, beside the channel, and it asks you to turn your back on the view most people came for.
