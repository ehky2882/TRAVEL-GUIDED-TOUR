# MIAMI W2 · 01 — South Pointe, facing north
W2 · Ocean Drive · segment 01 · S3 · plants the walk facet · TTS-safe · 24 September 2026

---

Put the channel behind you for this one and face north, up the length of the island. The ships are what most people come here to watch; this walk needs the other direction, the cluster of glass towers rising between the park and Fifth Street.

Those towers stand where they do because of a line on a map. When the Beach's historic district was listed on the National Register in May nineteen seventy nine, its boundary ran roughly along Sixth Street, taking in the blocks from there north to Twenty third Street and from the ocean west to Alton Road. The streets you are facing, south of the line, were left outside it, so this is where the high rises could go, while a few blocks north, across an ordinary cross street, the buildings drop to three and four stories.

The skyline in front of you works as a diagram, with the tall part showing roughly what much of the southern Beach might have become and the low part showing what the line kept. Even so, the protection was thinner than it looks from here, and the Beach spent the next fifteen years finding out how thin.

Follow the path north along Ocean Drive to Fifth Street, where Lummus Park opens on your right and the next stop, the hotel row, begins on your left.
