# MIAMI W1 · 02 — The Courthouse Over the Courthouse
W1 · The Pitch · segment 2 · S6 · clean · 24 September 2026

---

Find a spot on the south side of West Flagler Street, far enough back that the columned base and stepped tower fit into one look.

This building sits on top of another one. A county courthouse had stood on this ground since 1904, a stone building with a cupola, and the people who built it expected it to serve for about fifty years. By 1925, barely twenty years in, the county had outgrown it, and the new tower went up over the old courthouse, which was then pulled down. When it opened in 1928 it housed city hall as well as the courts, with cells high up. Its architect worked out of Atlanta, and he adapted a design he had once drawn for a city hall there that was never built, so a landmark of this skyline began as another city's unused plan.

Look at the columns and the tower climbing out of them. The base says old, settled and lawful; the height says new and in a hurry. That pairing was the whole message a young county wanted to send.

*[beat]*

A city that could outgrow a fifty-year building in twenty kept the habit. This tower held the courts for ninety-seven years, and in December of 2025 they moved into a new glass tower nearby, leaving a building grown too small for them in its turn.

The walk goes next to the Cultural Center, the plaza raised a story above the corner across Northwest First Avenue.
