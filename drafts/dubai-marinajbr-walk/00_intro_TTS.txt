Walk W4: Marina & JBR — An Evening in Two Waters
Segment 00 — Introduction
(TTS-safe version)

This walk has a correct time of day. Come at the end of it — the last hour of light, when the towers start switching on and the whole district comes outside.

Here's the idea. Within a few hundred metres of where you're standing, Dubai keeps two entirely different waterfronts. One is a canal the city dug for itself — still, inward-facing, lined on both banks with towers and dinner tables, water that behaves like a mirror. The other is the sea it was given — open, outward-facing, with sand and waves and a horizon. The first is where the district promenades; the second is where it breathes. They meet at a seam of footbridges near the marina's mouth, and crossing from one to the other in a single evening is, quietly, one of the best things you can do on foot in this city.

The practicalities are kind. The route is level and paved the whole way — a stretch of the marina's western bank, the crossing, then the beachfront boulevard — a little over three kilometres in total, no steps you can't avoid, and by the hour I've suggested, the heat has mostly gone off duty. Water on at least one side of you the entire time.

Find the promenade at the water's edge on the marina's western bank, anywhere near the towers, and face the canal.

That's Marina Walk. Start there.
