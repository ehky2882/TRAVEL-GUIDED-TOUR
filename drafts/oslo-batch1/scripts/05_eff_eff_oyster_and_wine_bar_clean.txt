Eff Eff Oyster and Wine Bar

Norway has one of the longest and most ragged coastlines in Europe, so you might assume Oslo had always been full of oyster bars, and you'd have been wrong, at least until 2019, when this one opened its doors.

Fredensborgveien twenty-two A, a quiet residential street a short walk north of the city centre, is the address, and the small bar at street level, with tables outside when the weather allows, is the place you're after, opening at four on weekday afternoons and at two on Saturdays, while Sundays depend on what the bar posts on its Instagram.

The idea came from Colin Duignan, originally from Vancouver, who noticed that Oslo had no proper oyster bar despite all that coastline, and he opened Eff Eff with his partners Aksel Steen and Torstein Voksø Eek.

There are three menus, one for oysters, one for food and one for drinks, and the oysters themselves come from Norway, when they're in season from October, as well as from France and Canada, with Champagne and crisp French whites doing most of the pouring, and a kitchen that leans towards cold dishes, which rather suits a place built around the raw and the briny.

It's a nice reminder that it often takes a newcomer to spot what everyone else has stopped noticing, because the oysters were always out there along the coast, and it took someone used to a different shoreline to ask why nobody in Oslo was serving them.

[beat]

If you're in town from October onwards, when the Norwegian oysters return to the list, order one alongside a French oyster and see how much a stretch of cold northern water changes the taste.
