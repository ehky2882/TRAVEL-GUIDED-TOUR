# Shanghai batch 1: where to find images

Every venue has three kinds of link.

1. **Free to use**: Pexels, Unsplash, Openverse (public domain only) and Wikimedia Commons searches. The image picker already holds what these returned and what passed review.
2. **Commons categories**: every file about the place on Wikimedia Commons. Most are **CC BY-SA**, which the app cannot credit (`CLAUDE.md` § Image Pipeline). Use one only with an owner OK plus a `drafts/CREDITS.md` line.
3. **Press and project pages**: the best photos of each venue, almost all by professional photographers (Pedro Pegenaute for Neri&Hu, for example). **They are copyrighted. Do not upload them.** Ask the venue or architect for press images, or use them only to recognise the right building.

Searches are in English and in Chinese. Xiaohongshu and Dianping carry the most recent visitor photos, but those are not licensed either.
