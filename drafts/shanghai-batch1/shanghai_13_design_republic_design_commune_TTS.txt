ATLAS · SHANGHAI · 13 · Design Republic
---
This building started life as a police station. Now people come here to buy chairs.

You're on jyahng ning Road, at number five eleven, in jing ahn. In front of you is a red brick building with a band of plain glass along the street, where you'd expect a row of shopfronts. This is the Design Republic Design Commune. In Chinese, it's shuh jee gong huh.

The building went up in the nineteen tens as a police headquarters, built by the British when this was part of the International Settlement. By the time the Shanghai architects nairy and hoo took it on, in twenty ten, it was almost derelict. The wood was rotten and the plaster was falling off.

The outside was protected, so most of it had to stay as it was. Look at the brick. The architects cleaned it, repaired it, and left it bare. Then look at the glass at street level. That's where a row of small shops once stood. They were removed and replaced with one long sheet of clear glazing. You can see straight through it to the old brick and the rough concrete inside.

Once inside, look for the cuts. nairy and hoo removed bits of floor, wall and ceiling in small, precise openings. Through them you can see old wooden laths and older brickwork, so you can read the building's history in layers, like rock in a cliff face.

Then the shop itself. Design Republic is a furniture shop that lindon nairy and rozzahna hoo started themselves. When the building reopened in twenty twelve, it became their flagship, with a gallery, a café and a restaurant around it. It even has a small flat upstairs.

So the architects took a building that had been about control, a police station, and opened it up. You can see in through the glass and through the walls. And the architects were also the shopkeepers, which is a nice way of making sure the building works.

If you've enjoyed their work, nairy and hoo's buildings are all over this city. The Blue Bottle café in jahng yooen and the lobby of the sook oh tie hotel on way hi Road are both about a fifteen minute walk south east of here.
