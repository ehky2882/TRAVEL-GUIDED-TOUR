# 13 · Nykteri's Cocktail Bar

## Clean script

Girona has a cocktail bar with a cocktail named after its flower festival, and you are standing outside it.

You are on Carrer de Santa Llúcia, a short street on the west bank of the river, a few minutes' walk from the Plaça de la Independència. Look for number four. It is a small room, which is rather the point.

Nykteri's opened in twenty thirteen. It was founded by Mariona Vilanova, who has more than twenty years' experience mixing drinks. It is an intimate space, with classic cocktails, signature creations and a gin list of more than fifty.

Two drinks here tell you a lot about the city. One is called Temps de Flor, after the festival of the same name, when every May Girona fills its courtyards, stairways and churches with flowers. The other is a line of cocktails made with ratafia, the local herbal liqueur of Catalonia, made by steeping green walnuts and herbs in spirit. The bar uses one from a producer called Terrània. Ratafia is the sort of thing a grandmother keeps in a cupboard, and here it is in a coupe glass.

What I like about it is that a good cocktail bar ought to taste of where it is. A lot of them, the world over, could be anywhere at all. This one pours the region's flowers and its walnuts into the glass. On Thursday evenings there is usually a themed after work session with three drinks and a bite, which is a very civilised way to end a day of sightseeing.

[beat]

After a drink, walk down to the Plaça de la Independència, with its arcades and its tables. It's the nicest place on this side of the river to stay out a little longer.
