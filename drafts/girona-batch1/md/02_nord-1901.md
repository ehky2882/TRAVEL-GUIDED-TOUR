# 02 · Nord 1901

*307 words · about 123 seconds at an unhurried pace*

## Clean script

Behind this rather sober street front there is a swimming pool, and in the middle of an old city that is a genuinely rare thing.

You are on Carrer Nord, a narrow shopping street on the west bank of the Onyar, a few minutes' walk from the river. Find the hotel's entrance, and notice how little it gives away. A townhouse front, a discreet sign, and that is your lot.

The hotel takes its name from its street and its date, and the building it occupies was completely restored in two thousand and nine. It is run by the owner's family, and you can feel that in the scale of it: rooms, a few suites, and a handful of apartments in the old town nearby, rather than a long anonymous corridor.

The gift is out the back. The rooms on the inside of the block look over a walled garden with a pool, and the hotel describes it, quite fairly as far as I can tell, as the only garden with a swimming pool in the city centre. In a town where most of the old streets are barely wider than a car, a private green courtyard with water in it feels close to extravagant.

And here's the thing. Girona's centre is full of these hidden backs. The street gives you one face, the courtyard gives you another, and the city keeps its best rooms out of sight. This is simply one of the few where a stranger gets to sit in one.

[beat]

From here, walk east to the river and cross by the Pont de Sant Agustí, the pedestrian bridge just upstream. Halfway across, turn around and look back at the west bank. You will see how the whole of this side of town sits low and flat, waiting for you to climb the hill opposite.
