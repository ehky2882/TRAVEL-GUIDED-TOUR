# 04 · Pensió Bellmirall

*308 words · about 123 seconds at an unhurried pace*

## Clean script

This is a guesthouse with thick stone walls and a painter's eye, tucked into one of the quietest lanes of the old town.

You are on Carrer de Bellmirall, a narrow street just behind the cathedral and on the edge of the old Jewish quarter, the Call. Look for number three. The front is old, plain stone, which is exactly the point.

The guesthouse was set up in the nineteen sixties by the painter Isidre Vicens, who lived from nineteen eighteen to two thousand and sixteen and was, by most accounts, something of a free spirit. It has kept its own character for more than fifty years, and his paintings still hang in its rooms. The rooms themselves keep their original stone walls and old furniture. It feels less like a hotel than a house that happens to take in guests.

Have a look at the street around you. These lanes were laid out in the Middle Ages, and they twist because they were built to fit the hill rather than to be easy to walk. The houses are tall, the light comes down in slices, and the stone is warm in colour. Staying here, you wake up inside the very fabric the rest of the city comes to look at.

What I like about a place like this is that it has not been smoothed over. A lot of old town hotels strip a building back to bare stone and add a designer's touch. This one simply carries on being what it was, a small family guesthouse with a painter's pictures on the walls.

[beat]

Before you move on, walk to the end of the street and up to the city wall. A stretch of the wall walk runs very close by, and from up there you can look straight across the cathedral roof to the hills beyond.
