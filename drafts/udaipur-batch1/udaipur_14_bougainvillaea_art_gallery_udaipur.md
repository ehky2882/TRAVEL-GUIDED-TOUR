# Bougainvillaea Art Gallery

**Udaipur, India** · tour 14 of 24 · slug `bougainvillaea-art-gallery-udaipur` · ~264 words (~1.8 min)

Udaipur is famous for its miniature painters, who work at the scale of a postage stamp. This gallery shows what happens when the city's artists decide to go large.

You're out on the northern edge of the city, on the highway near Kavita. The gallery is a bright, modern space, a long way in spirit from the antique shops of the old city.

The person behind it is the sculptor Bhupesh Kavadia. He's a Udaipur artist whose own stone carvings stand in public spaces in the city. He and his gallery have become a focal point for Rajasthani artists who want their work taken seriously, as art rather than as souvenirs for the tourist streets. It has been running for many years now. Travel writers regularly call it the most established contemporary gallery in the state.

Have a look at what's on the walls. The exhibitions rotate, so I can't tell you exactly what you'll find. Expect painting, prints, photography and drawing, and sculpture in marble and bronze. Some of it will be by well-known Indian names. Some will be by artists you won't have heard of yet.

*[beat]*

And here's the thing about seeing contemporary art in this city. Udaipur's palaces are full of extraordinary art, but almost all of it is centuries old. This is one of the few places where you can see what Rajasthan's artists are making now.

When you've finished, the Lily Court restaurant is right next door, built round a pond of water lilies. It's a good place to sit and argue about what you've just seen.
