# Bougainvillea Terrace by the Lake

Somebody decided a rooftop restaurant should feel like a greenhouse. On the shore of Fateh Sagar, it rather works.

You should find yourself on the roof of Hotel Lakend, on the eastern side of the lake. The water is spread out in front of you, with hills behind it on three sides. Around you the walls are covered in bougainvillea, pink and magenta. That's where the place gets its name.

Look at the planting first. The hotel describes the terrace as an open greenhouse, with the outdoors brought indoors, and that's exactly how it feels. The flowers do the decorating. There's very little else competing with them, which is the right decision with a view like this.

Then look out at the water. Fateh Sagar isn't quite what it seems. There was a lake here in the seventeenth century, but its earthen dam was washed away in a flood. In eighteen eighty-nine Maharana Fateh Singh built a new masonry dam, the Connaught Bund, named for Queen Victoria's son, the Duke of Connaught, who visited that year. The lake took the Maharana's name. You can pick out its islands from here. The largest is Nehru Park, and boats run out to it from the shore.

*[beat]*

What's worth knowing, sitting up here with a drink, is that this lake isn't scenery to the city. It's one of Udaipur's main sources of drinking water. The view and the water supply are the same thing.

When you leave, walk the Pal, the long promenade along the dam on the lake's eastern edge. It comes alive in the evening, with street food and half the city out to watch the sunset.
