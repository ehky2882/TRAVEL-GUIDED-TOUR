# Sunset on the Fateh Sagar Pal

**Udaipur, India** · tour 21 of 24 · slug `fateh-sagar-sunset` · ~294 words (~2.0 min)

Every evening, half of Udaipur comes to this embankment to watch the sky change colour. It's well worth joining them.

You're on the Pal, the long stone embankment along the eastern side of Fateh Sagar Lake. The water is in front of you, with the Aravalli hills behind it and the sun dropping towards them. Behind you are the food stalls and the traffic. Out on the lake are the islands.

Let's start with what you're standing on. This embankment is the dam that makes the lake. The first lake here was built in the seventeenth century, but its earth dam was washed away. In eighteen eighty-nine Maharana Fateh Singh rebuilt it in stone. He named the new dam the Connaught Bund, after Queen Victoria's son, who happened to be visiting. The lake took the Maharana's own name. Locals simply call the embankment the Pal.

Now the islands. The biggest one, with gardens and a café, is Nehru Park, and boats go out to it from here. Another has a white domed building on it, and that's not a pavilion. It's the Udaipur Solar Observatory, one of India's research observatories for studying the sun. It's built on the water because the lake keeps the air above it steady, which makes for sharper pictures of the sun.

*[beat]*

There's something rather nice in that. Scientists picked this lake to watch the sun by day. Every evening, the rest of the city comes to the same lake to watch it set.

Before you go, do what everyone else on the Pal is doing and eat something. Street food is part of the ritual here, so follow the longest queue. Then walk the full length of the embankment as the lights come on across the water.
