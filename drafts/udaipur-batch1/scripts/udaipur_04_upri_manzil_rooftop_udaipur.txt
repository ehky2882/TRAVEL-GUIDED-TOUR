Upri Manzil Rooftop — Udaipur, India
Vantage: Hanuman Ghat · 24.580435, 73.679810
---

Upri Manzil means, quite simply, the upper floor. In this part of Udaipur, the upper floor is where everybody wants to be.

You're near Hanuman Ghat, on the western shore of Lake Pichola, just south of the Chandpole bridge. Climb to the rooftop restaurant and face east, across the water. The long white mass on the far shore is the City Palace. Out on the water, to your right, is the Lake Palace, which seems to float.

Start with the lake. It's older than the city around it. The story goes that Maharana Udai Singh the Second enlarged it after he founded Udaipur in the fifteen fifties. In doing so he flooded a village called Picholi, which gave the lake its name. So under the water you're looking at, there's a village.

Now the food, which the restaurant keeps deliberately simple. It's local dishes and familiar ones, and the menu changes every few months. Regulars point you to the gatte, little gram-flour dumplings in a spiced yoghurt curry, a Rajasthani staple. Nobody comes up here for theatre on the plate. The theatre is across the water.

[beat]

And here's the thing about this side of the lake. The famous postcard of Udaipur, with the palace walls rising out of the water, is taken from roughly this shore. Over there you're inside the history. Over here you get to look at it.

Come back after dark if you can. The palace lights come up one floor at a time and stretch out across the water. From the Chandpole bridge, just to your north, you can watch the whole shoreline light up.
