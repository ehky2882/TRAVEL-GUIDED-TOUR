Ladu — Udaipur, India
Vantage: New Fatehpura, Panchwati · 24.602514, 73.689970
---

Ladu is the name of a shop, and it's also an acronym. It stands for Life Around Daily Utilities.

You'll find it at the Tapri Niwas tea house, on the main road in New Fatehpura near St Mary's School. Ladu is the shop on the same site. Look for shelves of small, considered things rather than a wall of souvenirs.

Have a look at the labels as you browse. Ladu stocks products from small makers, with a preference for things that are local, handmade, reusable and made in small batches. You'll find tea blends, spices, homeware and the odd piece of art. Its founder, Ankit Bohra, set it up in Jaipur alongside the Tapri tea houses. The idea was to give new small businesses a platform they couldn't afford on their own.

He described one rule in particular. Every maker gets the same amount of space and the same visibility on the shelves. There's no prime position you can pay for, no brand that shouts over the others. In a shopping centre, he pointed out, the big names always get the best spot. Here they don't.

[beat]

So a shop that looks like a nice place to buy a present is quietly doing something a bit more radical. It treats the smallest maker on the shelf exactly like the largest.

When you've chosen something, take it next door and have a chai at Tapri Niwas. The two were built to go together.
