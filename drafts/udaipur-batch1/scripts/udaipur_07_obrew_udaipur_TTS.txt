Oh Brew

There's a café on a hill in Oodaypoor where you can see a lake out of both sides.

You're at Ambaavgur, up the steep road above Svaroop Saagar, near the Hilltop hotel. Oh Brew is on the upper floors above the guest house there. Go up to the roof terrace, and the city spreads out below you, with water catching the light either side of the ridge.

Start with the view, since it's the reason the place was put here. From this height you can read how Oodaypoor is built: a string of lakes, dammed and linked over several centuries, with the city filling the gaps between them. Down at street level you'd never guess. Up here it's obvious.

Then the coffee, which is what the guide that sent you here rates above everything else. It promises the best for miles. Regulars describe it as floral and gentle rather than bitter, the sort of cup that tastes of the bean rather than the roast. There are wood fired pizzas too, if the climb has left you hungry.

What I like about Oh Brew is how understated it is. In a city where so many views come with a palace attached, this one comes with a flat roof, a few tables and a good cup of coffee. That turns out to be plenty.

Stay until the lamps come on across the city. Then take the walk back down slowly. The road down the hill gives you the lakes one more time, from a different angle, at every bend.
