The Lily Court — Udaipur, India
Vantage: NH 76, Kavita · 24.686769, 73.638594
---

Out on the edge of Udaipur, past the last of the traffic, there's a restaurant built round a pond.

The Lily Court sits off the highway north of the city, near Kavita. It's an open-air space with planting and seating arranged round a simple stretch of water. If you're standing at the edge of it, you should see water lilies on the surface, and quite possibly a swan or two looking unimpressed.

Have a look at how little there is. The water is the centrepiece, and everything else, the tables, the paths and the planting, is there to frame it. On a still evening it reflects the sky and the lamps. That does more for the room than any chandelier would.

The menu wanders more than the setting. Diners talk about khow suey, the Burmese coconut noodle soup, alongside Awadhi biryani, kebabs and a mezze plate or two. It's an eclectic list for a quiet garden. But this is a city that has always taken in travellers, so perhaps that's fitting.

[beat]

The interesting bit is the company it keeps. Just next door is the Bougainvillaea Art Gallery, often described as the most established contemporary gallery in Rajasthan. People who come for dinner tend to wander through it, and people who come for the art tend to stay for dinner.

Before you sit down, take five minutes in the gallery next door. Then come back for the pond just as the sun goes, which is when the lilies and the light look their best.
