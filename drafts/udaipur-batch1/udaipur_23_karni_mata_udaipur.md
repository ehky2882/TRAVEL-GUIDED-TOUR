# The Climb to Karni Mata

**Udaipur, India** · tour 23 of 24 · slug `karni-mata-udaipur` · ~286 words (~1.9 min)

**Vantage / pin:** Jawahar Nagar, Pichola — `24.565923, 73.689196` ([Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Karni+Mata+Mandir,+Udaipur/@24.5659231,73.6843247,17z/data=!4m14!1m7!3m6!1s0x3967ef86015f7f91:0x3d5bbb5ddff6cbc4!2sKarni+Mata+Mandir,+Udaipur!8m2!3d24.5659232!4d73.6891956!16s%2Fg%2F11cm3s30yl!3m5!1s0x3967ef86015f7f91:0x3d5bbb5ddff6cbc4!8m2!3d24.5659232!4d73.6891956!16s%2Fg%2F11cm3s30yl))

There's a cable car to the top of this hill. This is the other way up.

You're at the foot of Machla Magra, the steep hill that rises east of Lake Pichola, where the path begins. It's best walked early, before the heat. Above you, at the top, is the temple of Karni Mata. Most visitors ride up from Doodh Talai on the ropeway. You're going to earn it.

As you climb, keep turning round. It's the best reason to walk rather than ride. First you'll see the rooftops of the old city close below you. Then, as you gain height, the palace on its ridge, the shine of Lake Pichola, and finally the hills ringing the whole valley. On a winter morning there's often mist sitting in the low ground, and the city comes out of it a roof at a time.

At the top is the temple of Karni Mata, a form of the goddess Durga, much loved across Rajasthan. Just to be clear, this isn't the famous Karni Mata temple at Deshnok, out in the desert near Bikaner, the one with thousands of sacred rats. This one is quieter. What you'll notice first is the incense drifting down the path to meet you.

*[beat]*

And here's what I find rather lovely about this climb. The ropeway gets you the same view in a few minutes. Walking, you watch the city slowly assemble itself below you, street by street. By the time you reach the top, you understand how Udaipur fits together.

At the summit, find a spot facing the lake and wait for the sun to clear the hills. Then, if your legs have had enough, take the ropeway down. Nobody will tell.
