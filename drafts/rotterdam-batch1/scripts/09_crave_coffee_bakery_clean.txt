Crave Coffee & Bakery

The croissants in the window here contain no butter at all, which, in a country this fond of dairy, is a fairly bold thing to attempt.

Tucked into Vogelenzang, a short street in the old Maho quarter behind the Hoogstraat, Crave is the small café with the pastry counter right up against the window. The streets around you are small and tucked away from the big shopping routes, so the bakery feels more like a neighbourhood shop than a city-centre café.

Everything on the counter is plant-based, and that is the whole point of the place. Crave opened in the summer of 2023, set up by Yunlai Ye, who also runs the Tea Lab and Backyard, and the team spent months testing recipes before the doors opened, because making a flaky, layered pastry without butter is much harder than it looks.

The pastry to try is the cruffin, a croissant baked in a muffin shape, and the pistachio version is the one regulars keep coming back for. There are dark chocolate babkas and cardamom rolls too, and the coffee comes from Rotterdam roasters, with oat milk as the default rather than the alternative.

Crave's real ambition is not to prove that vegan baking is worthy, but to make sure nobody notices the difference. The founder has said he wants plant-based eating to feel easy and normal, and a bakery is a rather sly place to make that argument, since most people judge a croissant long before they ask what is in it.

[beat]

Take your pastry and wander a couple of minutes towards the Markthal and the old harbour, where the city opens up again. Or stay put by the window, which on a quiet morning is one of the nicer places in town to watch this neighbourhood wake up.
