Phil

This cafay calls itself your favourite neighbour, which is a bold promise to make on a street already full of neighbours.

On the Stadhowdersvekh, at number ninety six D in the leafy district of Blydorp, Phil is the cafay and shop sharing one bright room. The wide street is lined with the solid brick housing of Blydorp, a district laid out largely in the nineteen thirties, and the zoo and the parks lie just beyond.

Phil's coffee comes with an Asian twist, so alongside the usual espresso there is an iced blueberry hohjeecha, made with roasted Japanese green tea, and an iced yuzu americano, with plant based milks on offer. The food carries the same idea, with an avocado smash topped with feta and kimchi, a pairing that sounds unlikely and turns out to make perfect sense on a slice of toast, and ah sah ee bowls with granola for anyone who wants something lighter.

The space used to be a cafay called Breakfast for seven years, and in twenty twenty five it was renovated and reopened as Phil, a lifestyle shop and cafay rolled into one. Along with the coffee there is artwork by the artist Rookee, second hand fashion from the Slowdown Store, and design pieces and gifts.

Phil's real trick is how much of a neighbourhood it fits into one room. You can have breakfast, buy a present, pick up a jacket someone else has already loved, and leave with a print for the wall, which is very much the way a good local shop used to work.

When you have finished, find your way to the Vroosenpark, one of the most loved green spaces in this part of town, or to Deergahrda Blydorp, the city zoo, which is only a short walk away.
