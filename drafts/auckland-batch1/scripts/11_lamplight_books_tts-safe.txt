Lamplight Books

In twenty twenty three, a bookshop that had been open for barely two years was named the best in New Zealand, and it's the one you're looking at.

Parnell Rise climbs away from the city here, and at street level in the Guy zer Building you'll find Lamplight Books, behind a shopfront that glows on a dark afternoon. Inside, the shelves are stacked in square blocks reaching towards the ceiling, picked out in red, yellow and blue against dark, inky walls.

Those primary colours are a nod to the Bow house and to Mon dree ahn, and the dark walls were chosen to make the room feel calm and inward, a bit of respite from the visual noise that any shelf of book jackets makes. Somewhere among them is a small blue reading nook, which the judges singled out when the shop won Bookshop of the Year.

The owners, Melanie Oh Lochlin and Courtney Smith, were booksellers at Unity Books before setting up on their own, and they opened in June twenty twenty one, between lockdowns, which took a certain amount of nerve. The aim, in their words, was a beautiful, browsable space filled to the ceiling with books for the neighbourhood, and dogs are welcome too.

The building around them has a story of its own. The Guy zer Building opened in twenty twelve as the first in New Zealand to earn a six star Green Star rating, with an outer skin that traps warm air in winter and opens up to let the breeze through in summer. A low energy building is a fitting home for the most low tech pleasure going, which is wandering between shelves without knowing what you'll leave with, and that pleasure is what the award was really recognising.

Once you've found something to read, carry on up Parnell Road, past its run of restored Victorian and Edwardian shopfronts, and look out for the side lanes and courtyards tucked between them.
