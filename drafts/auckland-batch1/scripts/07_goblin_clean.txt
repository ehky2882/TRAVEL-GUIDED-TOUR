Goblin

Ponsonby has plenty of bars you can see from the pavement, and Goblin, quite deliberately, isn't one of them.

At the corner of Ponsonby Road and Richmond Road stands an old brick building with shops at street level, and the door you're after is on the Richmond Road side. Goblin is upstairs, in what its own owners describe as the rafters, so all you'll see from down here is the entrance and, after dark, a little light from the windows above.

That door has history of its own, since it's the same one regulars used for The Golden Dawn, a much-loved Ponsonby bar that once traded on this corner. Goblin calls itself a reimagining rather than a sequel, and The Golden Dawn's people helped promote the opening, which tells you something about how the place sees its inheritance.

Upstairs, the look is what the team calls neo-medieval and post-fantasy: low, flattering light, old-world furniture, and a series of rooms that feel like a hidden tavern rather than a cocktail lounge. The sound system was built by hand in Wellington, there's a dance floor, and the bar pours cocktails on tap, Hallertau beer, and a wine list given over to women winemakers.

Behind it is a small group of Auckland creative types, including Hamish Steptoe and the hairdresser and property owner Stephen Marr, with Jack Mancer looking after hospitality and Sam Harman, a DJ, running the music. They describe it as an upstairs island for those who seek it, and that phrase gets close to the appeal, because on one of the busiest corners in Ponsonby they've built a room you only find if someone tells you where to look.

[beat]

Goblin opens from late afternoon on Wednesday to Saturday, so if the door's shut, wander a few metres down Richmond Road, where the old villas and corner shops of Grey Lynn begin, and come back once the lights are on upstairs.
