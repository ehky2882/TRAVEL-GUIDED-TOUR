Meh tee tah

This restaurant is named after a woman who once ran a pancake stall in Samoa, where her son learned to cook.

The Grand by Sky City stands over you at ninety Federal Street, with the Sky Tower rising above the block behind. Meh tee tah occupies the ground floor corner that used to be the Italian restaurant Goosto, and the windows reveal a long, warm room with woven baskets hanging from the ceiling.

Those baskets are traditional Pacific fishing baskets, strung up across the dining room as its main decoration, and they set the tone for an interior by the design studio Control Space that its owners call a modern Pacific oasis. There's a private dining room too, the largest at Sky City, which seats twenty two.

The chef is Michael Meredith, one of the best known names in New Zealand cooking, who grew up in Samoa and named the restaurant after his late mother, Meh tee tah. It opened in October twenty twenty three as his first restaurant devoted entirely to Pacific food, with ingredients such as pork hock, pah ooah and fire roasted too ah too ah, and with South Pacific rum running through the cocktail list.

On Sundays, Meh tee tah serves a long lunch called toh oh nah ee, after the Samoan custom of a big shared family meal once church is over. Meredith's stated aim is to make Pah see fee kah food more accessible, and putting it at the heart of the city, inside a hotel and casino complex where visitors from everywhere pass through, is a fairly direct way of doing that; this is Auckland's own regional cooking, served at its busiest address.

When you leave, walk up Federal Street, which has been turned into a shared lane lined with restaurants, and at the top, look straight up at the Sky Tower, which from this angle seems to rise almost directly over your head.
