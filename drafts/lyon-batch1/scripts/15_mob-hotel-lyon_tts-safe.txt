The kitchen is ninety five per cent organic, and the company is a certified B Corporation, which means it's been independently assessed for its social and environmental record. The hotel calls itself a place to live in common, open to everyone, and it means it. People who aren't staying come for the restaurant, the bar and the outdoor spaces.
