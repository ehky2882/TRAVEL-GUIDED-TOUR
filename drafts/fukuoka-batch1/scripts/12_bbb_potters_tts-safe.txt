three bee Potters

The name is easier to remember than it looks: brew, bake and boil.

The shop is in Yahkoo een, filling the ground and first floors of its building. Step inside: the idea is that you pick things up.

three bee Potters sells tools for everyday life, and the three Bs stand for its motto: brew, bake and boil with Potters. Kitchen and tableware, food and drink, things for the bathroom, the bedroom and the garden, children's things, stationery. Everything is chosen to be practical, long lasting and well designed, at prices that don't make you nervous.

Have a look at how you're meant to shop. You're encouraged to hold things and feel the weight before you decide, which is how you'd want to buy a pan you'll use for twenty years. There's a sister shop nearby, three bee and, for the most everyday things.

What's worth knowing is that this sort of shop is part of what makes Yahkoo een feel the way it does. It's a neighbourhood where people live, shop and cook, and the stores here sell to them rather than to visitors. That's why it's worth an hour.

When you're done, walk a few minutes to Reck Coffee by Yahkoo een Station for a flat white, or wander north into the shops of Dyemyoh.
