B·B·B POTTERS

The name is easier to remember than it looks: brew, bake and boil.

The shop is in Yakuin, filling the ground and first floors of its building. Step inside: the idea is that you pick things up.

B·B·B Potters sells tools for everyday life, and the three Bs stand for its motto: brew, bake and boil with Potters. Kitchen and tableware, food and drink, things for the bathroom, the bedroom and the garden, children's things, stationery. Everything is chosen to be practical, long-lasting and well designed, at prices that don't make you nervous.

Have a look at how you're meant to shop. You're encouraged to hold things and feel the weight before you decide, which is how you'd want to buy a pan you'll use for twenty years. There's a sister shop nearby, BBB&, for the most everyday things.

What's worth knowing is that this sort of shop is part of what makes Yakuin feel the way it does. It's a neighbourhood where people live, shop and cook, and the stores here sell to them rather than to visitors. That's why it's worth an hour.
