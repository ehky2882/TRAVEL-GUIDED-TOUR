Try not to look up. It won't work — there's a building here that makes a fool of anyone who decides to be casual about it, and the district knows it.

Which is the frame for this walk. Downtown Dubai isn't really a neighbourhood. It's a room, and it has been furnished around a single object. The lake, the lawns, the terraces, the long curve of the boulevard — all of it exists to give people somewhere to stand while they look at one tower. We're going to use three of those places, in the order that makes the most sense of them: a lawn that gives you distance, a stretch of water that gives you a reason to stay after dark, and a bridge that gives you height. Three stops, one subject — unusual for a walk, and entirely appropriate here.

Practical matters first. The loop is short — comfortably under two kilometres, level, paved, and you can avoid every set of steps if you want to. What it doesn't have is shade. This is a district of open sky and pale stone, and the last hours of daylight are the version of this walk that works best, which happens to be when the fountain wakes up anyway.

Start on the mall side of the lake and look for the footbridge running out to the low green island in front of the tower. Cross it.

That's boorj park, and it's where we begin.
