It's a protected example of twentieth century architecture now. For a long time, though, people found it hard to love, the way brutalist concrete often is.

Here's the thing worth noticing. Most art schools are built to be seen. This one was built to disappear into its hill, so that the people inside could get on with making things. It still feels rather like a secret.
