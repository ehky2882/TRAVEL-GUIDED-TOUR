PHILADELPHIA — WALK 5: THE HOUSE THAT ISN'T THERE — SEGMENT 00: INTRO AT CARPENTERS' HALL
Walk 5 · segment 00 · register AMERICAN
Vantage: the court off Chestnut Street at the 320 block, facing the hall
---
Stand at the back of this court, with the brick hall in front of you and the traffic behind. You have walked in off Chestnut Street, past a row of signs that would like you to go the other way. They are not wrong. They send people west to east, from the glass building at Sixth and Market, down past the hall with the steeple, and out along Chestnut toward the river, and the day that route describes is a good day. This walk takes it backward. It starts here, in a court most people reach at the end, and it moves west, against the flow, toward the one stop on the route that has no building on it.

There is a reason for the order, and it is the reason for the walk. Going the signed way, the founding is a set of rooms you enter: a bell under glass, a chamber with green tables, a smaller chamber with a gallery. Going this way, the rooms come first and the ground comes last, and by the time you reach the corner of Sixth and Market you will have spent an hour beside buildings that are still standing, which is the only honest way to arrive at one that is not.

So: six stops, a little over half a mile, all of it flat, all of it on public sidewalk. Nothing here needs a ticket to be seen from outside; two of the buildings can be entered if you want, and the walk will say where that costs you a reservation and where it costs you nothing but a line. The route is Chestnut Street west for three blocks, a turn north at Sixth, and a stop at the corner. You will cross Fifth and Sixth at the lights and nothing else.

One instruction before the first stop. This hall sits back from the street on purpose; the builders who owned it wanted a yard, and the yard is still theirs. Hold the corner of it a moment and get the street out of your ears. The walk begins with an old kind of ownership, a building held by the trade that made it, and it ends on a corner where the building is gone. Make for the hall.
