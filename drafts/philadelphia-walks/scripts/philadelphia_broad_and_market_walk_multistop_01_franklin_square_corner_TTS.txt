philadelphia, walk 2: broad and market, segment 27: franklin square, northeast corner
Walk 2 · segment 27 · register AMERICAN
Vantage: The northeast corner of Franklin Square, under the bridge approach, Vine Street ahead
---
The roadway above you is the approach to the Benjamin Franklin Bridge, built across this corner of the square in the nineteen twenties, and the one you can hear but not quite see is the Vine Street Expressway, sunk in a trench along the north side of Vine Street and finished in nineteen ninety one. Between a bridge ramp and a highway cut, the square sits as the thing that was here first, which it was, by well over two centuries.

The trench matters to this walk because it runs west from here through the middle of Chinatown, and for the next four blocks you will see it at the end of every cross street: a slot of fast traffic where a neighborhood used to continue. The walk never crosses it. It keeps to Race Street, on the south side of the square, and turns away at Tenth. There is a plan to cap three blocks of the trench with a park, and the plan has had money and lost it and is looking for more; the city states it as a plan, and so does this walk.

Look west along Race Street before you set off. It drops under the bridge ramp, comes out into daylight, and runs straight into Chinatown where Chinatown began, on Race Street in the eighteen seventies, not on Arch. The gate you are walking toward was put up a century later at the neighborhood's southern border, not at its heart. Keep that in mind when you get there.

Walk west on Race Street for four blocks to Tenth, and turn left.
