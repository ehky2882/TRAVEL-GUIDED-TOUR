PHILADELPHIA — WALK 5: THE HOUSE THAT ISN'T THERE — SEGMENT 15: CONGRESS HALL
Walk 5 · segment 15 · register AMERICAN
Vantage: the northeast corner of Sixth and Chestnut, facing the building across the street
---
Stop at the corner here, on the Chestnut side, and take in how little there is. Two stories, brick, a few bays wide, built between 1787 and 1789 as the courthouse for Philadelphia County, which is what it looks like. It had been a courthouse for a year when the federal government arrived from New York in 1790, needed a building for its legislature, and took this one. Congress sat here for the ten years the capital was in this city. The House of Representatives had the ground floor; the Senate had the floor above; the county got its courthouse back in 1800 and used it for another seventy years.

Those are the facts, and the walk has stopped here for one of them in particular. In March of 1797 the president, finishing his second term, came to the ground-floor chamber to watch a man he had served beside for two decades take the oath as the second president, and then he left the building and, within days, the city, and went home. It had not been done before. Nobody on the floor knew for certain it would work. It was done in a county courthouse, in the smaller of the two chambers, in front of the representatives, because that was the room that was free.

That is what the building is for, now. Not the chambers themselves, though both are restored and you can see them with a ranger, without a ticket, if the door is open and the line is short, but the size. The people who ran this country for its first decade did not build themselves anything. They sat in a courthouse, slept in rented houses, and when the capital moved south in 1800 they took the papers and left the furniture. Everything on this walk so far has been a building the founding happened inside of. This is the last one. The next two stops are a bell that was taken out of its building and a house that is no longer standing, and between them the walk changes from looking at rooms to looking at what rooms leave behind.

Cross Chestnut at the light. The glass wall directly across from you is the south end of the bell's building, and the bell is behind it. Wait on the sidewalk in front of the glass.
