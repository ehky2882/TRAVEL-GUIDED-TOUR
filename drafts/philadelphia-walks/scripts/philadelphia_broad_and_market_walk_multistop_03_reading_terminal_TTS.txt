philadelphia, walk 2: broad and market, segment 08: reading terminal, from twelfth and arch
Walk 2 · segment 08 · register AMERICAN
Vantage: The corner of Twelfth and Arch, the north end of the train shed on the left, the convention center bridge overhead
---
The wall on your left is the back of a train shed, and the shed is the reason the street you have not yet reached is called Market.

Here is the argument this walk is named for, in full, once. The street a block south of here was laid out as the widest in the plan and called High Street, and for a hundred and seventy years the city sold its food in sheds built down the middle of it, so many sheds, for so long, that people stopped saying High and said Market, and in eighteen fifty three the name was made official. Six years later the sheds were torn out, the market moved indoors, and the street kept the name of a thing it no longer did. This is where the indoor version landed, under a railroad: in eighteen ninety two the redding company built its terminal on top of the market rather than move it, so the trains came in upstairs and the butchers stayed down.

What matters for a walker is where the middle went. In seventeen hundred the middle of Philadelphia was at the river end of this street. By eighteen hundred it was around Fourth. By eighteen fifty the sheds reached nearly to here. By nineteen hundred the city had built its hall a mile from the river, and the middle had officially moved to Broad Street. Nobody decided this. The middle moved the way a crowd moves, by each person stepping a little west, and the street called Market is the record of it.

The last trains left the shed in nineteen eighty four. In nineteen ninety three it became the convention center's great hall, and the market beneath it kept its hours through both. The bridge over your head joins the old hall to the new one across Arch Street, so that the building, like the street, has been quietly extending itself in the only direction left.

Stay on Arch to the end of the shed, turn left on Twelfth, walk one block south to Market Street, and turn right. The building with the tower is three blocks ahead. Enter it through the archway in its east face.
