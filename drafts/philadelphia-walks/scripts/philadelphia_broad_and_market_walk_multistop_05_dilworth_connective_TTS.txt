philadelphia, walk 2: broad and market, segment C1: dill wurth park (connective)
Walk 2 · segment C1 · register AMERICAN
Vantage: Dilworth Park, outside the west portal of City Hall, heading northwest toward Fifteenth Street
---
The park you have stepped into is Dilworth Park, opened in twenty fourteen on what had been a sunken concrete plaza from the nineteen seventies. It does two jobs at once: it is the roof of the subway concourse underneath, and it is the city's front lawn. In warm months the flat granite in front of you is a fountain you can walk straight through; in cold months it is an ice rink, with a cabin beside it and a crowd on it. Either way, keep to the right and make for the park's northwest corner, where Fifteenth Street meets the first block of the Parkway.

Cross Fifteenth at the light. You are about to enter the next plaza from behind its sculpture, which is deliberate.
