philadelphia, walk 2: broad and market, segment 29: love park, from behind
Walk 2 · segment 29 · register AMERICAN
Vantage: Entering love Park from Fifteenth Street, the sculpture seen first from its back
---
From this side the sculpture is four shapes on a stand, and for a second it is a sculpture before it is a word. The letters are there, but reversed, as if the word were looking at itself in a mirror. Walk around to the front and it becomes the word everybody knows, the right way round.

The sculpture is a loan that stayed. It arrived in nineteen seventy six as a temporary piece for the country's two hundredth birthday, was taken away two years later when the city declined to buy it, and was bought and brought back within weeks by a Philadelphian who thought the plaza looked wrong without it. He was right, and the plaza had a problem he had just solved: it had been built in the nineteen sixties as the formal start of the Parkway, which you can see running away to the northwest toward the museum on its rise, and for a decade nobody had been able to say what the plaza was for. The sculpture told them. The plaza was rebuilt in twenty eighteen, with less concrete and more lawn, and the sculpture was repainted and set back on the same spot, because by then the spot was the only fixed thing about the place.

Leave the plaza by its southwest corner, onto Sixteenth Street, and walk south two blocks to Market.
