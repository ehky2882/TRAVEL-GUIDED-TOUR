PHILADELPHIA — WALK 5: THE HOUSE THAT ISN'T THERE — SEGMENT 13: CARPENTERS' HALL
Walk 5 · segment 13 · register AMERICAN
Vantage: the court, facing the south front; interior optional
---
A guild built this, and a guild still owns it, which makes it the one building on the walk that has never changed hands. The Carpenters' Company of the City and County of Philadelphia, a society of master builders that goes back to 1724, put up its hall between 1770 and 1774 and has been collecting rent on parts of it, on and off, ever since. That is what to look at first: not a monument, a landlord's building, cross-shaped in plan, two stories of brick with a cupola, set back from Chestnut so the tenants had a yard.

The tenants are the story. The second floor went to a subscription library — the one the printer and his friends had started in 1731 — which kept its books here for seventeen years and let the delegates downstairs read them. The first floor went, for seven weeks in the fall of 1774, to fifty-some men from twelve colonies who had been offered the State House two blocks west and chose this instead, because the State House belonged to a government and this belonged to a trade. After them came a national bank, then a state bank, then the customs service, then a college of pharmacy, and in between, always, the carpenters, holding their meetings upstairs while the country rented downstairs.

Go in if the door is open; it usually is, there is no charge, and the room on the ground floor is the room. Two of the chairs are the ones the delegates sat in. But the thing worth carrying out of here is on the outside, in the setback. Every other building on this route was built by a government for a government's work. This one was built by the people who did the actual building, as a place to meet each other, and they let the government in as a tenant, briefly, when it had nowhere else to go. It is a good first stop for a walk that ends on a corner where the government was also a tenant, and the landlord was a merchant, and the house came down.

Edge back out to Chestnut and turn left. The bank is one block west, on the same side, and you will see its columns before you see its door.
