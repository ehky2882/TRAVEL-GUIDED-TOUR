PHILADELPHIA — WALK 2: BROAD AND MARKET — SEGMENT 26: CHINATOWN FRIENDSHIP GATE
Walk 2 · segment 26 · register AMERICAN
Vantage: Tenth Street at Arch, approached from the north, then from under the gate looking back
---
Coming down Tenth Street from Race, you meet the gate from the direction it was not designed for. It was built to be read from Arch Street, by someone arriving from the convention center side, and from the north you get its back: the same colors, the same tiled roof, and none of the ceremony of a front.

Walk under it. Then stop on the far side and turn around.

From here the gate reads end-on down Tenth Street, the way a gate is supposed to, and over its roof, two blocks up, is the slot of the expressway you have been walking beside. The gate was finished in 1984, by craftsmen sent from the sister city in China that also supplied the tiles. The expressway was finished in 1991. For seven years the neighborhood had a front door and no back wall, and then it had both. The convention center, the long building a block to your left, took another slice of the neighborhood in 1993. Behind you, on Market Street, is the block where an arena was proposed in 2022 and, in January 2025, was not; what replaces that plan is still an open question, and this walk does not pretend to know the answer.

Turn west on Arch Street. Walk two blocks, to Twelfth, and the long brick wall with the arched end on your left is where the walk picks up its street.
