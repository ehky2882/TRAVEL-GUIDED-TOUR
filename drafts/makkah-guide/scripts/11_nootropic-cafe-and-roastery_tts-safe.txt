noo tropic cafe and roastery

A noo tropic is anything said to sharpen the mind, so this cafe has set itself a high bar before you've even ordered.

Noo tropic cafe and roastery is on al Hajoon Street, in the az Zaahir district north west of the mosque, and it is a neighbourhood cafe rather than a pilgrim stop. It is easy to miss, so keep an eye on the shopfronts as you walk.

Have a look at the room first. It is small and cosy, and regulars describe the atmosphere as calming, with traditional Gulf songs playing in the background rather than the usual cafe soundtrack. That one detail tells you who this place is for.

Then the coffee, which is the reason to come. It is a roastery as well as a cafe, so the beans are chosen and roasted here, and the drinks people talk about are the cortado and the lattes. The staff are happy to talk you through what they are brewing, and it is worth asking.

What's worth noticing is how much Makkah's coffee has changed. This is a city where coffee has always been served, pale and spiced, as an act of hospitality. A specialty roaster playing Gulf music while it pulls a cortado is a younger generation keeping both traditions at once.

From here it is a short trip along the Madinah Road to the Mecca Museum in the old al Zaahir Palace. Have your coffee first, and then go and see how the city looked before cafes like this existed.
