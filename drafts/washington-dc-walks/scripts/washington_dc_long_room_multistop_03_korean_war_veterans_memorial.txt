# The Long Room — Korean War Veterans Memorial

---

You should be at the western end of the Korean memorial, on the south side of the pool, with the stainless steel figures on your right and a long wall of dark granite beyond them.

Stay on the path. The figures are a patrol, nineteen of them, spaced out across a field of low juniper and granite strips, walking toward a flag. They are larger than life and dressed for weather, and they are moving the same way you are, east, toward the pool. The dark wall running alongside them is etched with faces taken from photographs of the people who served, and it is polished enough that the figures appear in it, which is how the nineteen come to look like more. The pool at the far end, under the flag, was rebuilt in 2022, and a wall of names was added around it then.

Set this one against the Wall across the water, because they were built within a few years of each other and they are opposite answers to the same problem. The Vietnam memorial went down into the ground and put the names first. This one stays on the lawn and puts the people first, as figures you walk beside. One is a surface. This is a company you join for a hundred yards. Both of them face the room, and both were put here so that the room could hold them.

[beat]

When you reach the flag, keep going. The path runs east along the south side of the pool, under the elms, and the next segment plays as you walk. The World War II Memorial is at the far end, where the water stops.
