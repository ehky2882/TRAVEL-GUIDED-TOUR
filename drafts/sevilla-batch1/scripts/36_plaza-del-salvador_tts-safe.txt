Seville's second church stands on the site of its first great mosque. Part of the minaret is still there.

The story starts in eight twenty nine. That's when the mosque of Ibn Ah dah bahs was built here, and for more than three hundred and fifty years it was the main mosque of the city. Only in eleven eighty two did the Al moh hads open a bigger one, where the cathedral now stands.

After the Christian conquest the mosque became a church. By the sixteen seventies it was falling apart. It was pulled down, and in sixteen seventy nine work began on the church you see now.
