# Tamatsukuri Onsen Yu-yu, Matsue

**Place:** 255 Tamatsukuri, Tamayu-cho, Matsue, Shimane, Japan (by the Tamayu River, in the middle of the hot-spring town)  
**Coordinate (WGS-84):** 35.41626, 133.01073 (exact: GSI geocode of 255 Tamatsukuri; replaces the earlier area pin)  
**Note:** Day bath. Listed hours 10:00–22:00 (last entry 21:20), closed Mondays (or the next day after a national holiday); adult admission listed at 500–600 yen, confirm before use  
**Length:** 297 words (about 119 seconds)

## Script (clean)

This is an onsen town named after jewels. Tamatsukuri means making jewels, and for centuries this valley produced the curved magatama beads you'll find in ancient Japanese tombs. So its public bath, of course, is shaped like one.

Stand by the Tamayu River, in the middle of the hot-spring town. You should see a striking, modern building among the traditional inns, with a bold, curving form that echoes the comma-like shape of a magatama bead.

Start with the architect. It's by Shin Takamatsu, from Shimane, this prefecture, famous for his dramatic, machine-like buildings in Kyoto and elsewhere. Here, he took the town's jewel as his theme. The whole building is a play on the magatama, the curled bead with a hole at one end, a shape that's been sacred in Japan for more than two thousand years.

Now go up, if you're bathing. The big baths are on the top floor, the fifth, and the tubs themselves are shaped like magatama. There are outdoor baths, a waterfall shower to massage your shoulders, saunas, and a lounge to recover in afterwards.

And look up as you come in. The glass roof over the entrance hall uses see-through solar cells, so it lets the daylight in and makes electricity at the same time.

[beat]

Tamatsukuri is one of the oldest hot springs in Japan, mentioned in records from the eighth century. What I like about Takamatsu's bath is that it doesn't try to look old. It looks back to something even older, the jewels, and makes them modern.

If you've time, walk along the river through the town. It's lined with inns and bathhouses, and it's a lovely place for an evening stroll after a soak.
