# LVWA Sports Bookstore, Shanghai

**Place:** 588 Qingyuan Ring Road, Yangpu District, Shanghai, China (just outside the Shanghai University of Sport west gate, in the Ping-Pong Academy Building)  
**Coordinate (WGS-84):** 31.31366, 121.52189 (area: point is Shanghai University of Sport; the shop is at its west gate. Move before use)  
**Note:** Open weekdays 8am–8pm, weekends and holidays 10am–6pm (Shanghai government guide, August 2025)  
**Length:** 343 words (about 137 seconds)

## Script (clean)

Inside a sports university's old table-tennis building, someone has built a mountain out of books, and you can climb it.

Find the bookshop just outside the university's west gate and step inside. You should be standing in a tall atrium, seventeen metres high, under a skylight, and in front of you, a great stepped mountain of shelves and platforms rising up the wall, all in deep blue and cement grey, dotted with round glowing lamps.

Start with the mountain. Its proper name is Mingyue Mountain, the bright moon mountain, and the main peak is thirteen metres tall. It's built in five stepped layers. Inside it are three caves full of books, and up its face a stair winds between four reading platforms. At the very top there's a round opening for light, like a moon.

The client asked the architects, Studio YUDA and Studio NOR, for a landmark in that atrium. So a shop on one floor turned vertical. The architects liked the idea that climbing a mountain and looking for knowledge are much the same activity.

Now look around the rest of the shop. There are fifteen smaller book mountains scattered about, which work as shelves, seats, a bar counter and even a lectern. And where does that particular blue come from? The designers said it came from the protective film peeled off a metal door, which they spotted against grey wall paint, and liked.

It opened in twenty nineteen as Shanghai's first sports bookshop: biographies of athletes, sports science, fitness guides, a café, and a lecture hall where well-known athletes come to speak.

[beat]

I love that it's at a sports university. Students who spend the day training their bodies can come here and climb a different sort of hill. Visitors have compared browsing it to hiking through a moonlit valley.

Before you go, climb to the top platform. Look back down across the whole shop. It's one of the few bookshops in the world where the view is part of the stock.
