# Patchworks BKK, Bangkok

**Place:** 293/2 Charan Sanit Wong Road, Bang O, Bang Phlat, Bangkok 10700, Thailand (near MRT Bang O)  
**Coordinate (WGS-84):** 13.79910, 100.51007 (address)  
**Note:** Café. Mon–Thu 8am–6pm; Fri–Sun 8am–11pm (Time Out, August 2025). Confirm before use  
**Length:** 290 words (about 116 seconds)

## Script (clean)

Bangkok has a lot of pretty cafés. This one is made of raw concrete and looks a little like a machine, and it serves very delicate French pastries.

Stand on the pavement outside, near the Bang O station, and look at the building. You should see a two-storey block of bare concrete, all hard geometric lines and angles, with a slightly industrial, almost steampunk edge to its details.

Start with the concrete. The building is by the Bangkok design studio Party/Space/Design, and it leans into the brutalist look, raw surfaces, heavy shapes, strong lines, rather than the pastel and plants of most of the city's cafés.

Now go in and look at the counter. The contrast is the point. The pastries are made with French techniques and a real focus on quality, with seasonal specials. Time Out picked out a Christmas matcha mousse, and a layered dessert of lychee, raspberry and rose. There's a modern brunch menu, and the coffee is roasted at three levels, dark, medium and light, so you can choose your style.

Then look at the hours, because they tell you about the place. During the week it's a daytime café, closing at six. But from Friday to Sunday it stays open until eleven at night, which makes it a weekend evening spot as much as a morning one.

[beat]

I like the cheek of the combination. Heavy concrete outside, light-as-air patisserie inside. It's a bit like a bunker with a very good pastry chef hiding in it.

Before you go, order something from the pastry counter and find a seat with a view of the concrete. Then head back to Bang O station, just a short walk away.
