# Cliff Coffee, Yandang Mountain

**Place:** Cliffside caves on the route from Fangdong to Lingyan, Yandang Mountain Scenic Area, Wenzhou, Zhejiang, China  
**Coordinate (WGS-84):** none: cave sites between Fangdong and Lingyan, not yet geocoded. Locate before use  
**Note:** Two venues (300 and 600 square metres) serving coffee and light meals; completed July 2024  
**Length:** 333 words (about 133 seconds)

## Script (clean)

Somebody looked at a cave in the side of a cliff, high above a valley, and thought: that would make a lovely café.

Stand on the path where it passes the cliff. You should see a sheer wall of rock, and set into a natural cave in its face, a café, mostly glass and metal, tucked so far back into the stone that from a distance it's hard to see where the cave ends and the building begins.

Start with that idea of hiding. The designers, Slow Coral Design, called their approach weak design: letting the mountain be the strong one, and keeping the building quiet so it does as little damage as possible to the cliff and the view. There are two of these cafés along the route, one of about three hundred square metres and one of six hundred.

Now go in and walk out onto the viewing platform. It's the real reason for being here. Inside the cave mouth, there's a wide deck looking straight out over the peaks and gorges of Yandang Mountain, one of China's famous scenic mountains, known for its strange rock pinnacles and waterfalls. You sit at the edge of the cliff with a coffee or a light meal, with nothing but air in front of you.

The designers were also trying to solve a modern problem. Yandang Mountain is an old, traditional scenic area, and they wanted to bring in younger visitors, with something a bit fashionable, without spoiling the place. The cafés were finished in July twenty twenty-four.

[beat]

I like the honesty of the brief. A café in a national beauty spot could easily have been a concrete box with a car park. Instead, it's a cave you can sit in, which is how people have enjoyed mountains for thousands of years.

Before you go, carry on along the route to Lingyan, the Spirit Rock area. Its towering cliffs are among the most famous sights on the mountain.
