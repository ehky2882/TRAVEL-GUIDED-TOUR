# WeDoctor Green Valley, Hangzhou

**Place:** Information Port, Xiaoshan Economic and Technological Development Zone, Hangzhou, Zhejiang, China (between Jianshe 3rd and 4th Roads)  
**Coordinate (WGS-84):** 30.20487, 120.23877 (street level: Qidi Road. Move onto the central green valley before use)  
**Length:** 345 words (about 138 seconds)

## Script (clean)

From the street, this looks like a fortress of offices. Step inside the ring and you'll find a valley.

Start on the edge, on one of the main roads that bound the site. You should see a long, continuous wall of silver-white aluminium and tall glass, with a strong vertical rhythm, running unbroken along the street. That's deliberate. The architects, gad, led by Zhang Wei, call the approach tight outside, loose inside.

Now walk in from the north-south axis that runs through the middle of the scheme. The buildings step back and are cut away, and between them opens the green valley: a public space at ground level, flowing out to Information Port Park on the western side.

Look up and you'll see the valley carries on in three layers. On the ground, the park and the central valley. Above that, roof gardens on the stepped tops of the apartments and the shopping podium. And highest of all, what the architects call cloud platforms, slung between the office towers. Along the centre line there's a rounded connector, shaped rather like a flying saucer, cantilevered out on a curved steel truss.

The towers on the eastern side are arranged in a pattern based on the company's infinity-sign logo: two main towers and six more set symmetrically around the axis. At the foot of the main eastern building is a lobby with a clear span of thirty-nine metres, closed by a single, delicate net of cables holding up the glass.

[beat]

This is the headquarters of WeDoctor, a digital health company, and it's huge, around five hundred and eighty thousand square metres. Big campuses like this usually turn inward and shut the city out. The surprise here is how much of the inside has been given to anyone who walks in.

Before you go, find one of the X-shaped skybridges over the street and walk across it. From the middle, you can see the terraces stepping up on both sides, like a mountain range made of offices.
