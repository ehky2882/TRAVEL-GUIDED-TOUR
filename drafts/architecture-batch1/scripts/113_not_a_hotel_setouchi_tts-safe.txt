From the shore, look towards the south-west cape of the island. You should see low buildings following the curve of the hillside, with long roofs that bend and slope with the land, and earth-coloured walls, all looking out over the calm, island-scattered Inland Sea.

Start with the walls. They're rammed earth, made from soil dug out of this very site and pressed into layers, so the buildings are literally made of the hill they stand on. You can see the stripes of the layers, like a cutaway of the ground.

Now look at the roofs. They're covered in solar panels, and they twist and turn to follow the slope, so the buildings look less like boxes placed on the hill and more like ribbons laid across it.
