# Palazzo Moroni

*335 words, about 134 seconds at a natural pace.*

## Clean script

From the street it's a fairly sober front on a steep lane. Behind it is the largest private garden in Bergamo's upper town.

You're on Via Porta Dipinta, in the Città Alta. Go in through the entrance and stop in the courtyard of honour. In front of you is a nymphaeum, a grotto-like fountain, and above it the first of the gardens climbing away up the hill.

The palace was built in the middle of the sixteen hundreds by Francesco Moroni, to show the world how far his family had risen, and the Moronis lived in it for almost four centuries.

Climb the grand staircase and look up. The painter Gian Giacomo Barbelli covered it with the tale of Cupid and Psyche, and he went on to paint the main rooms. In one of them, the Hall of the Giants, the figures seem to break straight through the ceiling. Another tells the story of Jerusalem Delivered, the great Renaissance poem of the Crusades.

Then find the Hall of the Golden Age. It holds portraits by Giovan Battista Moroni, one of the finest portrait painters of the Renaissance, and the best known is the Knight in Pink, a young man called Gian Gerolamo Grumelli, signed and dated fifteen sixty. He stands there in rose-coloured silk looking entirely pleased with himself, and frankly he's entitled to.

[beat]

And then go outside, because the garden is the real surprise. Formal terraces climb against the hill of Sant'Eufemia, and above them is the ortaglia, almost two hectares of kitchen garden, meadow and fruit trees, added in the seventeen and eighteen hundreds. A working farm, inside the walls of a hilltop city.

The FAI took on the palace in twenty nineteen, entrusted to it by the foundation that had cared for it.

If you've the legs, climb right up through the ortaglia to the top. From there you look down over the roofs of the Città Alta, and you realise how much green this city has been keeping behind its walls.

## Fact-check notes

- The build date is FAI's 'a metà Seicento'. The 1636 date is unverified and not used.
- 'Entrusted' follows FAI's 'affidato'; the legal form isn't stated.
- The Knight in Pink being 'signed and dated 1560' is from the FAI teaser.
- 'One of the finest portrait painters of the Renaissance' is a general characterisation of G. B. Moroni, not from the FAI page.
