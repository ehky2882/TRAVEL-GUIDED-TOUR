From the inner courtyard of the castle at Manta, look up at the walls around you. Plain, heavy stone, built for keeping people out rather than inviting them in. On a clear day, the pointed peak behind you to the west is mon vee so.

That room is the Baronial Hall, and its walls were painted around fourteen twenty by an artist nobody can name. He's simply called the Master of the Manta Castle.
