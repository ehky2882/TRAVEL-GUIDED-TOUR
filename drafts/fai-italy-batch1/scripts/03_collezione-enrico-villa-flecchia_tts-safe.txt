Collezione en ree ko a Villa flek ya

Most collectors hang their landscapes facing a wall. This one hung them facing the landscape.

Villa flek ya stands on the edge of man yah no, on the low glacial ridge called the sair ra. Turn so the village is behind you and look out. Fields, woods and small villages run flat towards the horizon, and the only thing that breaks it is the long, level line of the sair ra itself, curving round the great glacial basin of ee vray ah.

The house is modern, built between nineteen fifty five and nineteen seventy, with simple lines and every window turned to this view. It belonged to the architect pyair oh en ree ko and his wife fran ka. In twenty eleven they gave it to the fy, with everything inside it, and pyair oh explained why: he wanted to preserve, over time, what he had collected with love and sacrifice over the course of his life.

What he collected was paintings, more than sixty of them, and almost all landscapes. His passion was for the painters who shaped art in peed mont from the late eighteen hundreds to the middle of the twentieth century, whether they were born here or simply chose it. Look for Antonio fon ta nay zee, the great Romantic landscape painter of peed mont, and Lorenzo del lay ah nee, who painted these same hills and mountains. Then walk on to Francesco men tsee oh, and to the abstract work of Luigi spat sa pan, and watch the landscape slowly come apart into colour and shape.

That's the quiet pleasure of this house. Inside, a hundred years of people trying to paint this part of the world. Outside every window, the world they were trying to paint.

Visits here are by arrangement and on set days, so do check before you come. And when you leave, walk up into man yah no itself. It's a small village of medieval origin, sitting right on top of the ridge, and from its edge you can see the sair ra from the inside.
