Fontana Pretoria, Palermo

This fountain was made in Florence, taken apart into six hundred and forty-four pieces, shipped to Palermo, and put back together. The people of Palermo still call it the Fountain of Shame.

You're in Piazza Pretoria, a few steps from the Quattro Canti, the crossroads at the centre of old Palermo. The fountain fills almost the whole square: tier on tier of marble basins, rails, steps and statues, with gods, river figures and animal heads spouting water.

It was carved in Florence in fifteen fifty-four by the sculptor Francesco Camilliani, for the garden of a villa belonging to a member of the Toledo family, the brother of the Duchess of Florence. In fifteen seventy-three he sold it to the city of Palermo. It arrived in May fifteen seventy-four in six hundred and forty-four pieces, a hundred and twelve of them packed into sixty-nine crates. Some were broken on the way.

Putting it back together took seven years. Camilliani's son Camillo did it, with the help of the sculptor Michelangelo Naccherino, and to make room, houses around the square were pulled down.

So, the shame. One reason was the cost, which kept rising. The other, the more famous one, is that so many of the figures are naked, and the nuns of the convent of Santa Caterina, right on the corner of the square, were said to be thoroughly scandalised.

[beat]

Have a look at the four groups around the fountain. They were renamed after the rivers of Palermo, among them the Oreto and the Papireto. And at the top, the Genius of Palermo, the city's protector, who started life in Florence as a Bacchus.

In seventeen thirty-seven an iron railing went round it to stop vandals, and it's been restored several times since, most recently between nineteen ninety-eight and two thousand and three.

When you've had your fill, look at what surrounds it. The town hall is on one side, the church of Santa Caterina on the other, and down the steps is the church of San Giuseppe dei Teatini. It's one of the great stage sets of the Baroque, and the fountain is the leading lady.
