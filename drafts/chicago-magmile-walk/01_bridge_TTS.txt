Daniel Burnum and Edward Bennett's Plan of Chicago, published in nineteen oh nine, proposed connecting Michigan Avenue to Pine Street with a bridge, then widening Pine to match. The concept was approved around nineteen thirteen, the street was renamed in nineteen seventeen, and construction started the following spring. It opened in May of nineteen twenty.
