The building was controversial. Traditionalists hated it. But in June nineteen thirty four Mussolini summoned the architects to Rome and praised it, and that settled the argument.

And here's the thing. A building made for arriving and leaving can still be calm, low and careful with its city. That was a radical idea then, and it's not a bad one now.
