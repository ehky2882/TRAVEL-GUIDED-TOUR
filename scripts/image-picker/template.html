Tap a photo to pick it. The first photo you pick for a tour becomes the hero. Each one after that joins the gallery, in the order you tap. Tap again to unpick. Use View to see a photo full size.

Every photo here passed two automated checks (a real modern photograph, and the right place), and then a look by eye. Codes like 03-5 match the numbered images in our chat.

Photos marked CREDIT NEEDED are Creative Commons and need a photographer credit, which we keep in the repo since the app has no credit line.

Your picks are saved in this browser as you go. When you're done, tap Copy picks and paste the summary to me.
