### 🔴 Why only one of the two heroes is a real photograph

The rule is that a place hero must be a **third** photograph — the fault found across 13 of the
first 24 places was one picture printed three times. Both of these places are built of two link pins
with no Atlas tour, so there was no third photograph in the catalogue to promote.

- **The Bonaventure got one.** `Westin_Bonaventure_Hotel-01.jpg` on Commons is **2816×2112 and
  public domain** — the single PD photograph of the building at usable size out of ~60 Commons
  files, every other one being CC BY or CC BY-SA. ⚠️ **The frame is a wide downtown skyline
  dominated by the US Bank Tower and a car park**, so the centred square the tool would take is a
  poor hero; it was hand-cropped to the cylinder cluster instead — a **0.85× downscale, not an
  upscale** — and the crop was then nudged up 120 px to lose the parked cars along the bottom.
- **Casa del Mar could not.** Commons holds exactly one real photograph of the building, 2950×2252,
  and it is **CC BY-SA 3.0** — off-policy while the app has no attribution UI. The only PD images
  are **596×346** and **539×331**, needing a **2.6× upscale**, well past the 1.50× high-water mark
  in the catalogue. Every other "casa del mar" hit is a Spanish-language book scan or a hotel in
  Santander del Mar. So it borrows `hotel-casa-del-mar-centenary-thedesigndetourist_hero.webp`, the
  aerial of the courtyard — an establishing shot of the property, though it carries the creator's
  own burned-in headline. **This is the Legion of Honor / Waterlooplein case, and the owner has
  closed it.**

⚠️ **The `--focus`-style hand crop was NOT applied to the Casa del Mar hero.** Re-cropping a
creator's frame to strip their headline is exactly what got the Castel Béranger re-crop reverted
(*"i dont want to editorialize other people's work"*). Recovering text the crop destroyed is
