## The decisions, and why

### The pin moves, never the tour — asserted, not assumed
All four Atlas tours are `geofenced`, so moving one changes where its audio fires. All four pins are
`manual`, so moving one costs nothing. The build asserts **both** halves before it writes anything.
Moves: **11.4 m** Casa Loma · **40.3 m** Distillery · **56.7 m** Osgoode · **9.5 m** the Crystal, onto
the ROM place's existing coordinate (whose two other members were already exact).

### 🔴 The Osgoode tour's coordinate is a deliberate vantage — do NOT "correct" it
It sits **57 m south of OSM's `Osgoode Hall` node**, on the Queen Street fence. That is where the cow
gates are, and the cow-gate myth is the tour's entire hook. Grand Central / Petersen shape. The pin
was on OSM's node (`43.6521039, -79.3857945`, forward-geocoded by name) and moved to the tour.

### Every hero is a third photograph — and the build refuses otherwise
`hero not in {tour hero, pin hero}` plus `hero in tour.additionalImageURLs` are both hard assertions.
**Borrowed-hero count: 21 of 88 → 21 of 91** — derived on both bases, so this change adds none.
**All nine candidates were rendered and looked at.**

- **Distillery `_6` is a judgement.** The most *establishing* frame: the cobbled lane, the stone mill,
  the scale of the works, in daylight, two distant figures. Rejected: `_4` LOVE-padlock wall (warmer,
  no people, but **one wall**); `_5` Christmas market (the lane end to end with `GOODERHAM & WORTS`
  legible, but **seasonal** — the Grand Central trade-off, taken only when the alternative is a
  close-up, and here it isn't); `_3` The Ordinary shopfront with tourists; `_2` B&W Pure Spirits.
  ⚠️ `_6`'s `JOHN FLUEVOG` tenant sign is legible — a real shopfront in a re-tenanted Victorian works,
  the Crocker Galleria precedent, not a watermark. **One line swaps to `_4`.**
- **Osgoode's three candidates are ALL interiors**, because the tour's own hero already carries the
  classical exterior. `_2` (painted-ceiling hall, no people) over `_3`/`_4` (two courtrooms).
- **Casa Loma `_2` was the only candidate** — that tour ships a one-image gallery.

### 🔴 Membership was swept at 320 m, not taken from the checker's pairs
Correctly excluded: **Spadina Museum** 150 m (the Austin house next door, a separate museum),
**Nathan Phillips Square** 189 m, the **City Hall pin** 270 m, **Gardiner Museum** 111 m,
**Museum Station** 150 m.

**The one that needed care: the Old Town walk's stop 5 IS the Distillery District, 16 m away — and it
is correctly NOT a member.** A walk anchors on **stop 0**, which is **1,771 m** away at St Lawrence
Market, and that walk is already in the **Union Station** place; a tour may belong to one place only.
A raw nearest-stop distance reads as a 16 m candidate and is wrong.

### Addresses are editorial, corroborated rather than taken
- **Casa Loma** reverse-geocodes **by name**: *Casa Loma, 1 Austin Terrace*. ✅
- **Osgoode Hall** forward-geocodes **by name** at *130 Queen Street West*, on the pin's coordinate. ✅
- **Distillery District** ships **`55 Mill Street`**, the complex's own published address (38 m from
  the place), **not** OSM's `10 Trinity Street` filing — the La Pedrera rule. The tour's own hero is a
  street sign reading `TRINITY STREET`, so both are true of the site.
