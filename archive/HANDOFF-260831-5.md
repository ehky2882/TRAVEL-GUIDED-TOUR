## 🔴 Windsor is the point of the exercise

Session 127 deliberately spread six Windsor pins across **three** coordinates (0 / 143 / 376 m)
because `TourSetMap.maxStacked = 3` would otherwise have left three of them **unreachable on the
creator's own maker page**. That spread was a workaround for a cap.

**A place collapses its members into ONE capsule pin, so the cap stops applying** — and all six go
back onto the castle where they belong. The same reasoning applied at Hampton Court (3 pins, 64 m)
and Blenheim (2 pins, 123 m). **This is the durable lesson: when coincident pins are being spread to
dodge the stack cap, a place is the real fix and the spread is the stopgap.**
