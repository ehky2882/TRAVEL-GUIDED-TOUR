## 🔴 THE WEBSITE HAD SAID "COMING SOON" FOR ELEVEN DAYS

Found while adding the download link the owner asked for. **The App Store listing gives
`dozent.world` as its support URL**, so anyone tapping through from the listing — a Stripe reviewer
with round 5 in review included — landed on a page saying the app did not exist yet.

Six places, three pages (#825):

- The splash, where it was the entire message.
- **Both of the splash's meta descriptions** — what every shared link and social preview has shown.
- The About status card: *"in its final review with the App Store and is not yet publicly
  available"*, plus *"more than 1,400 tours across 31 cities"*, stale on both numbers. Re-derived
  live: **1,553 tours, 116 cities, 22 countries**.
- **`/g/`, twice** — the page someone lands on when a friend shares a group-listen link and they do
  not have the app. **The worst possible place to be told to wait for a launch that already
  happened.** Its "Notify me" mailto is now a download link.

**The badge is Apple's own artwork, committed unmodified**, as their guidelines require — not
redrawn, not restyled, not set as live text. The **white** variant, because every page sits on
`#000000`. Links are **storefront-neutral** (`apps.apple.com/app/id6771030927`, no `/us/`) so a
visitor lands in their own country's store; the catalogue covers 22 countries.

- **⚠️ A bug caught by rendering, not by reading.** Inside the About card the `inline-block` badge

  tours are free to listen to."* in half across the artwork. It is `display: block` inside `.card`
  now and the card's prose is wrapped in `<p>`; the CSS comment says why, since the splash still
  wants `inline-block`. **The markup read fine — only the picture showed it.**
- **Verified live after deploy, not assumed:** all three pages serve, zero "coming soon" remain, and

- **⚠️ Pre-existing, NOT fixed here:** the `/g/` card clips at the right edge on a narrow viewport.
  `origin/main`'s version was rendered to check — **identical before and after**, so it is not a
