## Two of my own tools were wrong, and are now committed so they stop being rewritten

Both are hand-written from memory in session after session, and **both were wrong
again on rebuild today**. They now live in `scripts/`.

* **`scripts/decode-plus-code.py`** — its first run put **all seven Hong Kong
  codes in THAILAND**. `recover_nearest` took the missing prefix from the *total*
  character count when it must come from the count **before the `+`**. Caught
  only because Hong Kong is not in Thailand. Self-tests three published anchors,
  the floor-not-round encode, refusal of grid digits, 2,000 round-trips and 18
  short-code recoveries across four hemispheres — every expectation derived from
  a real encode, never recalled. Now has a CLI and selftests before answering.
* **`scripts/validate-tours-mirror.py`** — refused to run **twice**, both times
  correctly, both times its own bug. A bare `case manual` in Swift has an
  **implicit** raw value, so an enum parser matching only `case x = "y"` reads an
  **empty domain and then passes anything**; and the duration field is
  `totalDurationSeconds` / `audioDurationSeconds`, not `durationSeconds`. Both are
  pinned by selftest cases. ⚠️ It is a stand-in, never the authority — CI's Swift
  validator decides.
