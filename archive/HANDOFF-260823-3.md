## 1. The coordinate fault, measured — [PR #562](https://github.com/ehky2882/TRAVEL-GUIDED-TOUR/pull/562)

**Owner: "fix the coordinate issue at the source."** The generator is outside this
repo, so it could not be patched from here — but it can now be identified and caught.

**The fault is real, upstream, and measured.** Reverse-geocoding every supplied point
and comparing against the matched object's own centre, point-like matches only:

| city | sourcing | north/n | median | p |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| New York | old / manual | 10/23 | −1.4 m | 1.0 |
| London | old / manual | 14/28 | −0.4 m | 1.0 |
| Barcelona | drop pipeline | 13/18 | +10.2 m | 0.096 |
| Milan | drop pipeline | 28/32 | +10.3 m | 1.9e-05 |
| **pipeline combined** | | **41/50** | **+10.3 m** | **5.6e-06** |
| **old-sourced combined** | | **24/51** | **−0.9 m** | **1.0** |

**🔴 THE OLD-SOURCED CITIES ARE THE CONTROL AND THEY ARE DEAD CENTRED.** That is what
proves the measurement is unbiased rather than the method having a northward tilt —
run that control before believing any bias result.

**The gross errors are the same fault at a different scale.** A constant offset in
**screen pixels** becomes a larger ground distance the further you zoom out, and every
magnitude fits one ~20 px upward offset: `+10 m → z19` (one building) · `+262 m → z14`
· `+653 m → z13` (rural) · `+3,200 m → z11` (another town). **Which is why the worst
errors are always the subjects furthest from the centre — those are the ones you zoom
out to find.** Owner confirmed the coordinates are picked **by hand, by his brother**,
which fits exactly: clicking a map pin's body or label rather than its tip. Advice
given: right-click the exact spot and copy the coordinates from the menu.

**⚠️ The +10 m in the shipped catalog is deliberately NOT corrected** — inside phone GPS
error, and rewriting ~1,000 coordinates is more risk than the defect.

**`scripts/check-coordinates.py`** (Automation Rule #8b) reports GROSS / UNVERIFIABLE /
BIAS. **⚠️ A distance alone proves nothing** — four Milan coordinates looked 200 m to
9.4 km wrong and were correct (a same-named district, a same-named street in another
comune, two street centroids), so a GROSS verdict requires the geocoder to have
actually found the venue. Four traps, each found by running it against the raw drop
and finding it wrong: a city hint silences out-of-town venues, but dropping the hint
matched **Chad** and **Somaliland**; compound folder names must be split; resemblance
must match the hit's **name, not its address**; and a point inside the venue's own
footprint is fine (Castello Sforzesco is 180 m square) **with a 30 m tolerance, because
the pipeline's own +10 m bias pushes points just outside OSM footprints.**

## 2. The same photo twice, and five wrong buildings — [PR #564](https://github.com/ehky2882/TRAVEL-GUIDED-TOUR/pull/564)

**Owner: "VELASCA TOWER TOUR - 4 IMAGES IN CAROUSEL, BUT MY APP SHOWS 2 IMAGES, BOTH
REPEATED."** Also Triennale (8 shown, 4 real) and Sidewalk Kitchens (6 shown, 3 real).

**Cause:** four Milan drop folders carried each photo twice — `.jpg` at the outer level,
`.webp` inside a nested directory — and the collector reads both levels *by design*.

**🔴 THE BYTE CHECK COULD NOT HAVE SEEN IT. A JPEG and a WebP of one photograph share no
bytes. A byte check is not a duplicate check.**

**Rebuilding it found five live wrong buildings nothing had ever caught** — all
re-encodings, so invisible to sha256: `plaza-de-santa-ana_hero` was the **Reina Sofía**;
`victoria-and-albert-museum_hero` the **Old Operating Theatre** and `_3` the **Gardner
Museum, Boston**; `st-lawrence-market_6` (Toronto) **LA's Grand Central Market**;
`old-royal-naval-college_5` the **Banqueting House ceiling**. Session 76 fixed
Thyssen == Reina Sofía; **Plaza de Santa Ana carried the same photograph and was missed
then.** Four of five were fixed free from the tour's own gallery — the Castello /
DuSable pattern, **four times in one day**. ⚠️ **The V&A's wrong hero was ALSO the
Albertopolis walk's stop-1 image — sweep for other references before calling such a fix
done.** Also fixed: The Charging Bull showed 5 images that were 3.

**⚠️ ONE STAGE IS NOT ENOUGH.** A bare average hash clustered the **Empire State Building
with the Pincio and a Naoshima sculpture** — similar tones. The hash now only nominates;
a 32×32 thumbnail comparison decides. **A checker that cries wolf gets ignored exactly
as surely as one that passes falsely.**

The tool also now uses **curl not urllib** (urllib fails SSL on this Mac — which is how
it printed *"OK — no suspicious duplicates"* having fetched nothing) and **exits 2 with
COULD NOT VERIFY** above a 20% failure rate.

## 3. Architects: 94 → 279 — [PR #565](https://github.com/ehky2882/TRAVEL-GUIDED-TOUR/pull/565)

**Owner: "Yes add the architects."** The backlog was recorded as nine from Milan plus a
few others; a catalog-wide sweep found **207 candidates**. 170 tours gained 193
architect tags, 138 gained `Designed by a Master`; **all 279 names are in use, 409 tours
name an architect, 0 missing the shelf tag.**

**24 rejected, and the rejections are the value.** A mention is not authorship: **Francis
Greenway** only *suggested* a Sydney harbour crossing in 1815; **James Stirling's**
Palazzo Citterio plans were **never built**; **Vertner Tandy** is a *resident* of
Strivers' Row; **Paul Renner** designed the *typeface* Futura. Nine more were already
present under another form — `I.M. Pei` vs `I. M. Pei`, `Kazuyo Sejima` vs `SANAA`,
`Taniguchi Yoshio` vs `Yoshio Taniguchi`. **Compare on normalised token sets, not
strings.**

**Editorial rule, written down for the next sweep:** include whoever designed the
building, a major part of it, its landscape or its complete interior scheme; exclude
anyone who made a single artwork inside it.

**🔴 `scripts/validate-tours.swift` KEEPS ITS OWN COPY OF THE VOCABULARY.** Editing only
`Models/Tag.swift` produced **193 validator errors**. Both are updated and asserted equal.

Validator 0/0 across 1,466 tours; **`test_sim` 418/418**.
