# Handoff — 2026-08-30 (session 122, tooling)

**The duplicate-image checker could not see a link pin.** Not "could not scope to a batch," which is
what six sessions in a row wrote down — it could not see one at all, `--all` included.

Branch `claude/tour-links-upload-qeoxe7`, restarted clean off `origin/main` at `f93321b6` after the
link-pin batch and the John Portman vocabulary change had both merged. Tooling + docs only: no

## What was actually wrong

`scripts/check-image-duplicates.py` was written in [#453](https://github.com/ehky2882/TRAVEL-GUIDED-TOUR/pull/453) /
[#455](https://github.com/ehky2882/TRAVEL-GUIDED-TOUR/pull/455) on **19 August**. Its `build_index`
iterates `catalog["tours"]` and nothing else.

[#597](https://github.com/ehky2882/TRAVEL-GUIDED-TOUR/pull/597) moved link pins out of `tours` into a
sibling `linkPins` array on **25 August**, because one unfamiliar `kind` inside `tours` fails the
whole catalogue decode on every build shipped before `TourKind.link` — and build 66 is at Apple and
is strict forever. The script was never taught about the second array.

Measured before the fix, against the live catalogue:

```
--all sees 5595 images
link-pin heroes in catalogue: 240
of those, seen by --all:      0
```

So `--all` had been reporting success over a set it was not fully checking, for five days and six
link-pin batches.

**⚠️ My own first description of this to the owner was wrong and is corrected here.** I told them it
was a convenience gap and that *"nothing in the catalogue is wrong because of it."* The scoping half
was true; the blindness was not something I had checked. The catalogue does turn out to be clean —
but that is now a measurement, not an assumption.

## The fix

Three changes to `scripts/check-image-duplicates.py`.

**1. `build_index` takes a scope.** New `entries_in_scope(catalog, scope)` returns `tours`,
`linkPins`, or both.

**2. `--pins` flag, and `--maker` deliberately left alone.** The obvious move — fold pins into
`--maker` — is a trap. `--maker` matches `displayName` as an uppercase substring, and pinned
creators' handles collide with city codes: **`--maker STO` matches `@urbanstoriesyt`,
`@hollywoodhistory`, `@naturalhistorymuseum` and three more; there are 31 such collisions across the
catalogue** (`AMS`→`@spacesofmydreams`, `BER`→`@WienerbergerAG`, `MEL`→`@meliluu__`, and so on). So
the scopes are explicit:

| flag | covers |
|---|---|
| `--maker <CODE>` | tours by that maker — **unchanged**, no pins |
| `--pins` | link pins; `--pins --maker <handle>` for one creator |
| `--all` | tours **and** link pins |

**3. `tour_slug` falls back to the hero filename.** A link pin hosts no audio — every one carries
`audioURL: ""` — and `tour_slug` derives its slug from that URL. Without a fallback all 244 pins
collapse onto one empty slug and `classify` cannot tell them apart.

`classify` gained a link branch. Byte-identical heroes across two pins is an **error**, and the
reasoning is worth keeping: a pin re-hosts its own post's thumbnail and nothing else, so two pins
that are the same clip cross-posted to two platforms (the Zacherlhaus case) are *two separate
downloads* and are never byte-identical — they surface perceptually, as INFO. Byte-identical means
one hero was written twice from one decode, which is the Thyssen bug wearing a link pin's clothes.

## Verification

- **Selftest 13 checks, all pass**, and **self-tested against 3 injected faults — 3/3 caught**:
  `--all` reverted to tours-only, the pin slug fallback removed, and a link-pin duplicate downgraded
  to INFO. A new `_selftest_scope()` builds a two-entry fixture catalogue offline and asserts each
  scope reaches exactly what it should, that the pin's slug is not `""`, and that `--maker NYC` does
  not leak the pinned creator in.
- **`--pins`: 240 images, 0 errors, 0 fetch failures, 10 seconds.**
- **`--all`: 5,835 images, 0 errors, 27 INFO, 0 fetch failures, 7 minutes.** 5,835 = 5,595 + 240
  exactly, which is also the proof that the tours scope is byte-for-byte what it was.
- **The perceptual pass was confirmed to have teeth on the new data** rather than silently
  no-opping: **165 pairs nominated at Hamming ≤ 45, every one rejected by the thumbnail
  confirmation, closest 23.0 against a threshold of 8.0** (identical pictures score under 1).
- `--maker CPT` returns 98 images, matching what CLAUDE.md records for Cape Town.

## Things a future session should know

- **`--pins` reports 240 images for 244 pins.** The five `@malata.antwerp` Italian-antique-market
  pins share one hero URL by design (one link resolved as five pins), so they are one entry in the
  set, not five.
- **Places are still out of scope, deliberately.** A place hero is allowed to be a member's own hero
  **at the same URL** — Legion of Honor, Hotel Casa del Mar and Oedo Antique Market all do this —
  which produces no group at all, and nothing else in the catalogue references a place image.
  Including places would surface those three deliberate borrows as errors.
- **`--all` is 7 minutes and `--pins` is 10 seconds.** That gap is the whole reason six batches
  worked around this by hand instead of running the tool. Reach for the narrow scope.
- **Nobody was working on it.** Three branches appeared to "touch" the script; all three are stale
  (last commits 13–21 August) and contain only the two commits that originally created it. Checked
  before starting rather than assumed.
