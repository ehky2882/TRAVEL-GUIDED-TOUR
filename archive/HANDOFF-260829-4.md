### 🔴 THE PLUS-CODE DECODER WAS WRITTEN BY HAND AND ITS FIRST ENCODE WAS WRONG

`pip install openlocationcode` still fails to build a wheel here, so
encode/decode/`recoverNearest` were implemented again. **CLAUDE.md's warning
about checking against official vectors rather than remembered ones was earned
a second time, in a new way:** three of the four "official" vectors written
into the first self-test were misremembered, and the code was right. The fix
was to derive every expectation from the specification's own pair arithmetic
(symbol index × 20°, 1°, 0.05°, 0.0025°, 0.000125°) and check it by hand.

**A real bug did surface once the vectors were correct.** The float encode loop
computes `0.375 / 0.05` as `7.4999…`, truncates to 7, and emits the wrong
symbol two pairs later — `7FG49QFG+X2` where the answer is `7FG49QGG+22`.
**Encode is integer-only now** (scale to 1/8000°, take `% 20` five times).
Self-test: three spec-derived decodes, two encodes, **2,000 random round-trips
asserting the encoded cell contains its point**, and four `recover_nearest`
round-trips across hemispheres.

Both supplied codes then reverse-verified onto their subject **by name**:
`P2QG+2W` → **Waterside Plaza**, Manhattan; `9XGH+RW` → **嘉道理碼頭 Kadoorie
Pier**, Tsing Bik Street, Castle Peak Bay, which is the street the caption's
Boulder Lodge story names.
