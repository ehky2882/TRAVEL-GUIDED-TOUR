## 🔴 A new failure class: a thumbnail TikTok's CDN will not serve

**13 of 95 posts** answer their oEmbed thumbnail URL with **52 bytes of
`{"code":5009,"error":"fail to make process filters"}`**.

- **It is not a dead post** — oEmbed returns full metadata (title, author, a thumbnail URL).
- **It is not transient** — 5 spaced attempts, all 13, all failed.
- **It is not a host or transform pattern** — working and failing posts share both
  `p16/p19-common-sign.tiktokcdn-us.com` and `tplv-tiktokx-dmt-logom`. It is **per object**.
- **`make-link-pin.py` behaved correctly**: it refused with `COULD NOT VERIFY` rather than
  shipping a broken hero.

**The fix: the SAME post's `originCover`, read off the post page.** It uses the
**`tplv-tiktokx-origin`** transform instead of `tplv-tiktokx-dmt-logom` and serves fine — and is
a better source anyway (no logo overlay, larger). It was fed in by **monkeypatching the tool's own
`best_thumbnail`**, so `trim_bars`, the square crop, the blurred 4:3 pad, the filenames and the ids
are all the tool's own code — `scratchpad/batch/fixthumb.py`. **13/13 recovered.**

**If this recurs, fold the fallback into `best_thumbnail` itself** rather than re-deriving it.
