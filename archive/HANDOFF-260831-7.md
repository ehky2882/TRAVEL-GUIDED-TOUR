## 1. What the byte checks structurally cannot see

Everything `check-image-duplicates.py` did before today is about **two different URLs holding the
same bytes** — the Thyssen bug, where a staging commit re-used an image decoded 40 seconds earlier
and wrote it under a second filename.

The Natural History Museum is the **opposite shape**: two entries pointing at the **same URL**. One
file hashed once is one file, so it never forms a group, and the check is blind to it **by
construction, not by oversight**. Nothing anywhere touched `audioURL` at all.

The cost of that blind spot, measured: **six and a half weeks** (2026-07-15 → 2026-08-31) of the
London tour playing Los Angeles' narration, with no error, no 404, every URL returning 200, and
`validate-tours.swift` reporting clean. It was found by a human reading a gallery.

## 2. What was added

Three functions in `scripts/check-image-duplicates.py`, plus a selftest and the wiring:

- **`asset_refs(entry)`** — every `(role, url)` an entry points at: `heroImageURL`,
  `additionalImageURLs[i]`, `stops[i].imageURL`, `stops[i].audioURL`.
- **`classify_shared_url(refs)`** — pure, so the selftest pins every branch with no catalogue.
- **`report_shared_urls(catalog)`** — prints findings, returns the error count.

The rules, in order, and the reason each one is where it is:

| | verdict | why |
|---|---|---|
| any holder role is `audioURL` | **ERROR** | audio is never legitimately shared — at most one of them can be right. **This is the NHM bug.** |
| all holders are link pins from ONE `sourceURL` | INFO | the `@malata.antwerp` case: five pins in five cities cut from one *Top 5 Italian antique markets* video, sharing its thumbnail by design |
| holders span two or more cities | **ERROR** | the signature of a slug collision — two unrelated places that happened to share a name |
| any holder is `multiStop` | INFO | a walk reusing the imagery of a landmark it passes, in its stop images or its gallery |
| otherwise | **ERROR** | two entries in one city share an image — a mis-stage |

**⚠️ The one-source-post rule is checked BEFORE the city rule, deliberately.** The malata pins are
legitimately in five different cities, so with the order reversed the documented case becomes five
false errors — and a checker that cries wolf gets ignored, which is the same failure as one that
says nothing (the founding lesson of this script's own rebuild).

**⚠️ An empty `audioURL` must never group.** Every one of the 483 link pins carries `audioURL: ""`,
so without the filter all 483 land in a single enormous bogus finding. Pinned by a selftest case.

**⚠️ An entry reusing its OWN asset is one entry, never a finding** — a single-stop tour setting
`stops[0].imageURL` to its own hero is the documented convention on 304 tours.

## 3. Two decisions worth not re-litigating

**🔴 The check is deliberately NOT scoped by `--maker` / `--pins`.** A collision is a property of
the whole catalogue: the NHM one spanned **two cities and two makers**, so any scope narrow enough
to be convenient would have hidden the exact bug the check exists to find. It runs on every
invocation regardless of scope. This costs nothing — it is pure JSON, no network, and adds
milliseconds to a run that otherwise fetches thousands of files.

**Places stay out of it**, for the same reason they are out of the byte checks: a place hero is
**allowed** to be a member's own hero at the same URL (Legion of Honor, Hotel Casa del Mar, and
every one of the fifteen borrowed heroes from tiers 1 and 2). Including them would produce a
finding per borrowed hero and nothing else.

## 5. What this does and does not close

✅ A bare-slug collision that puts one narration file or one photograph on two entries is now
caught the moment it enters the catalogue, catalogue-wide, with no network.

⚠️ **It cannot see a collision that has not happened yet in `Tours.json`** — specifically, it does
not know that a gh-pages *upload* is about to overwrite a live file. That remains the job of the
staging-time filename check (#567: a correction means a new filename, never new bytes at a live
URL), and the handle/city suffix convention that has now prevented live-hero overwrites in at least
eight separate batches.

⚠️ **It is not in CI**, matching the rest of this script — the byte half is network-bound and a
full run pulls thousands of files. The shared-URL half alone would run in milliseconds and is a
reasonable candidate for `ci.yml` later; it was deliberately not smuggled into a tooling PR.
