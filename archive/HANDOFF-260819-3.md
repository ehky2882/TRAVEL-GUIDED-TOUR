## Lessons

1. **Answer the literal question.** "What do you sell through Stripe?" is answered *"nothing"* —
   and that single word is what dissolves the Restricted Businesses concern. Do not describe the
   business as though Stripe processes its sales.
2. **The strongest fact was not the obvious one.** Merchant-of-record is the reflex answer;
   the **pre-publication human review gate plus the published Acceptable Use Policy** is the
   better one, because unmoderated content reaching buyers is what Stripe actually screens for.
3. **When a capability is absent, say so once and move on.** Browser control was unavailable this
   session; the owner was sure it had worked the day before. Four searches confirmed its absence,
   but **two explanations offered for why were guesses and both were wrong.** Five messages went
   to a question a 30-second screenshot answered. Verify, state plainly, propose the workaround —
   and do not theorise about causes that cannot be checked from inside the session.
