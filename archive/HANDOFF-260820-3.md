**Optional box** — used rather than left blank, since it is the only place to keep the correct framing in front of whoever picks the case up next. Four load-bearing facts:

1. **Not a travel reservation service** — no bookings, dates, seats or supplier inventory; nothing delivered at a future date. A purchase unlocks an audio file immediately. (Dozent's App Store category is Travel, and *"Travel reservation services and clubs"* is on Stripe's Restricted Businesses list right beside *"Content creation platforms"* — that ambiguity is why both readings get answered.)
2. **Apple is merchant of record for 100% of consumer purchases** (In-App Purchase). Stripe processes no cardholder payment and carries no chargeback exposure. This is the fact that kills every reading of the flag.
3. **Stripe's role is payouts only** — creators onboarded through Connect Express so we can pay their share. Stripe, not Dozent, is the regulated party; we are not a money transmitter.
4. **No transactions processed, no payouts made to date.** True regardless of activation state — the session-97 lesson, after *"our account is in test mode"* was read off a stale project note and nearly sent to a financial institution.

Plus links to the acceptable-use, terms and privacy pages.
