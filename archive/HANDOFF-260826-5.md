## 🔴 THE ELEVENTH LINK FAILS TWICE OVER, AND THE SECOND REASON IS NEW

`https://www.tiktok.com/t/ZP8vMRXW5/` resolves to
`https://www.tiktok.com/@carsons_room/photo/7353776356167109930`.

1. **It is dead.** oEmbed returns **HTTP 400 `{"message":"Something went wrong"}`** on three
   spaced attempts, and the page returns HTTP 200 carrying *"Video currently unavailable"* with
   **zero `og:` tags**. No caption, no thumbnail, no creator name — the same wall as the previous
   batch's tenth link, though that one returned an empty 200 shell rather than a 400.
2. **🔴 IT IS A `/photo/` URL, AND A TIKTOK PHOTO CAROUSEL CAN NEVER BE A LINK PIN.** Verified
   directly against the tool: `derivable_embed()` returns **`None`** for a `/photo/` URL and a
   player URL for the same id under `/video/`. The app builds its embed from `/video/{id}`, so a
   photo post would render a hero and never play — which is exactly what the tool's guard exists
   to refuse. **So even a live photo carousel is structurally unsupported today.**

**This is worth knowing before the owner sends more links: TikTok photo posts are not pinnable at
all.** Supporting them is a code change (the app would need a non-player detail path), not a
content one.

### ⚠️ TWO OSM WAYS ARE BOTH TAGGED "1529 VASSAR STREET", 170 m APART, AND THE FIRST RESULT IS ON THE WRONG STREET

Warehaus is at **1529 Vassar St** (its own listings). Nominatim returns **two** results for that
address — `way 77689379` and `way 156300351` — and **both are road segments**, so the house number
is *interpolated*, not a mapped building. Taking the top-ranked result would have put the pin at
**Lake Silver Elementary School on Rio Grande Avenue**, a different street.

Resolved by reverse-geocoding each candidate and reading the road back:

| candidate | reverse-geocodes to |
|---|---|
| 28.5726988, −81.4027875 | **Vassar Street, house 1557** ✅ |
| 28.5729111, −81.4010417 | Rio Grande Avenue — Lake Silver Elementary School ✗ |

**The lesson generalises: when a forward geocode returns more than one hit for one address, reverse
each candidate and check the ROAD comes back right.** A distance between them tells you nothing
about which is correct.

**⚠️ Two other pins reverse-geocode to a neighbour and are fine** — FAVO's point (718 Highland Ave,
its own stated physical address) reads back as *730* Highland, and Cromulent's (1647 E Colonial)
reads back as *1616* E Colonial. Same street, adjacent numbers, nearest-mapped-building noise.

**⚠️ Susuru was nearly put 15 km wrong by an assumption.** Its hashtags read like a Mills 50
restaurant and that is where I first placed it; it is actually at **8548 Palm Pkwy near Disney
Springs**, which OSM names exactly. Checked before it was written.
