## Editorial

- **⚠️ Three of the four places share a name with one of their own members, unavoidably.** 120
  Broadway could take a street address instead (the Rockefeller Center precedent); **One Times
  Square, The Tin Building and Eastern State Penitentiary have exactly one name each.** Do not
  "fix" this — the alternative is inventing a name the site does not have.
- **⚠️ Hero trade-offs, each one line to reverse.** **Scribner's** takes the painted flank-wall
  ghost sign (`CHARLES SCRIBNER'S SONS / PUBLISHERS AND BOOKSELLERS`) over Flagg's real 1913
  iron-and-glass shopfront, which reads as a generic clothing store at thumbnail size. **One
  Times Square** takes the tower under its billboards (a small sign-holder in the foreground)
  over a close-up of the crystal ball. **The Tin Building** takes the market frontage over a
  restaurant interior. All member heroes were rendered and looked at, not chosen by filename.
- **⚠️ Every figure was verified this session.** One Times Square: **25 storeys, 363 ft, Cyrus
  L. W. Eidlitz, 1903–04**, and **its original detail is gone** — the facade has been
  advertising since the 1990s, which is why the floors behind it stand largely empty; the ball
  has dropped since **31 December 1907**, every year but **1942 and 1943**. Tin Building: opened
  **1907** inside a market trading since **1822**, **one of two surviving market structures and
  the only one with landmark designation**, and **what stands today was reconstructed and raised
  after Hurricane Sandy** — a fact no caption carries and the copy states plainly. Eastern
  State: **John Haviland**, opened **25 October 1829**, **the model for more than three hundred
  prisons worldwide**, closed **1971**. Scribner's: **Ernest Flagg, 1912–13**, exterior
  landmarked **1982**, ground-floor interior **1989**.
- **⚠️ Eastern State's copy is unflinching and undramatised.** The separate system is described
  as what it was — a person meeting no one, left with nothing but penitence — Dickens is
  recorded as having left appalled, and the ruin is stated as deliberately stabilised rather
  than restored. No mortality figure, no true-crime register; Capone and Sutton named once,
  factually.
