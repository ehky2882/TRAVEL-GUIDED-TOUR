## The live catalog RPC is intermittently timing out

Found while answering "are tours live" — **worth someone's attention, and not caused by this
branch.** `POST /rest/v1/rpc/get_catalog` returned **HTTP 500 `57014 canceling statement due to
statement timeout` on 3 of 9 attempts**; the successes come back in 2–3 s and the failures die
at 4–9 s. The payload is now **10.5 MB** from Supabase.

✅ **It was seed contention, not capacity — established by re-probing rather than assumed.**
#739's `publish-catalog` seed was applying against the same tables throughout, and that job does
one large transactional upsert; once it eased, **8 of 8 further probes came back 200 in 2–3 s**.
So there is nothing to fix, but there is something to know: **a cold launch during a catalog
seed can silently take the fallback path.** The app degrades correctly — `RemoteCatalogLoader`
falls back to the gh-pages mirror, which served 200 on every single attempt.

⚠️ **The lesson is the sequencing: do not diagnose a live service while a seed of it is in
flight.** The first reading (3 of 9 failing) would have read as a capacity problem and been
reported as one. Check `publish-catalog`'s "Apply seed to Supabase" step before probing.

⚠️ **Also observed, and still true at the time of writing: #739's four places had merged but
were NOT yet live** — `main` carried 125 places while both the RPC and the gh-pages mirror
served 121, because that seed step was still running. **A merge is not live until the RPC serves
it**; the merge's green tick says nothing about propagation.
