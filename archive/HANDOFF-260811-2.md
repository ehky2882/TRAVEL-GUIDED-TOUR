## Branch inventory — audited by content this session

**36 `claude/*` branches audited; 30 are dead, 6 keep.** Full list + method in CLAUDE.md
§ "Branch inventory (authoritative, 2026-08-11)" under the Sydney block. Headlines:

- **Ancestry lies here.** Everything is squash-merged, so `git branch --merged main` lists
  nothing and `git diff main..branch` fills with reversals of newer main work. Compare
  **blob SHAs** of each branch's contribution-vs-merge-base against main; split UNIQUE
  (absent from main) from STALE (main superseded an older copy).
- **🔴 But `unique: 0` does NOT clear a branch — I wrote that rule down wrongly first and
  the cross-check caught it.** It only means "adds no new files"; a branch that *edits*
  existing files looks identical whether its edits merged or never merged.
  `architect-tags-yjw2k4` reports `unique: 0` and is entirely unmerged. **Always
  corroborate with merge status** (a documented merged PR, or the content provably live in
  `Tours.json` / `Tag.swift`).
- **⚠️ Re-derive before acting — the remote went 32 → 36 branches *during this session*.**
