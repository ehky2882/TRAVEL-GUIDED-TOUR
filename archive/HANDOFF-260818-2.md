## 🔴 The headline: ten wrong coordinates, all wrong in the same direction

Checked all 78 supplied coordinates against what each script actually describes, then reverse- and
forward-geocoded every venue. Ten land far enough off that **the geofence would never have fired** —
no error, no warning, no dead link, just a tour that does nothing while you stand in front of the building.

**Every one is displaced due north.** That is a systematic upstream fault in whatever produced the
coordinates, not random error — worth fixing at source before the next city, because the small ones are
invisible and the large ones are fatal. All ten corrected against OSM and **reverse-verified onto the
named venue**: OSM names 8 of them exactly, Walden 7 resolves to Carretera Reial 106 (its real address),
Teresianes to Ganduxer 85-105 (the address its own script names).

### What looked wrong but is not — do not "correct" these
- **Passeig de Gràcia houses sit 31–60 m EAST of their OSM nodes.** That is the opposite pavement, where
  you stand to look at a facade. Casa Batlló 39 m, Casa Amatller 60 m, Casa Lleó Morera 31 m.
- **Museu Tàpies is 74 m out, at the Aragó / Passeig de Gràcia corner** — which is also its walk stop, and
  where a walker heading north would naturally stop.
- **Sant Pau is 252 m from the OSM "Hospital de Sant Pau" node** because that node is the *modern* hospital
  behind; the supplied point is the Modernista entrance the script describes.
- **Cafè de l'Arquitecte and Hotel Casa Sagnier are 8 m apart** — the café is inside the hotel. Two genuinely
  distinct tours at building scale; `MapClustering.needsDisambiguation`'s building-scale backstop handles it.

### Two Nominatim traps that recurred
1. **A forward geocode returning a road with no house number is a street centroid, and its distance means
   nothing.** It flagged Dry Martini at 503 m when reverse-geocoding had already named the venue exactly,
   and Bar Colòmbia at 288 m when the real address check put it at **5 m**.
2. **Casa Calvet "failed" to geocode purely on query formatting.** Re-asked as `Carrer de Casp 48, Barcelona`
   it resolved at **19 m**. Re-query before concluding anything is wrong.

## Conventions applied

- **Eight tours ship outside Barcelona with their own `city`** (the Aït Benhaddou / Campinas convention):
  Montserrat ×5, Sant Just Desvern ×2 (Walden 7 and La Fábrica — Bofill's neighbouring pair, ~130 m apart
  once corrected), El Prat de Llobregat, Santa Coloma de Cervelló, Sant Cugat del Vallès, Mataró,
  Esplugues de Llobregat, L'Hospitalet de Llobregat, Sant Adrià de Besòs.
- **The Dreta walk's seven stop images are byte-identical to the seven singles' heroes**, so they were
  **not uploaded twice** — the walk stops point at the singles' uploaded files (the documented walk-reuse
  slot). The walk's own hero is a **unique** aerial of the Eixample grid supplied loose in the walk folder.
  Montserrat's walk stops carry their own 10 images, all referenced.
  **230 uploaded = 230 referenced, 0 orphaned** — the São Paulo lesson checked explicitly.
- **The Dreta walk's stop 0 sits on Casa Lleó Morera's exact coordinate** — the standard walk-intro-on-a-
  landmark coincidence, and a **place candidate**. Deliberately not created: a place needs its own copy,
  address and photograph, which is separate editorial work.
- **Sensitivity.** Hotel Neri's square was bombed in January 1938 and the shrapnel scars are left visible;
  the script says many of those killed were children sheltering inside. The description keeps that factual
  and unflinching and **carries no mortality figure**, per the Eastland / Harbour Bridge convention.
  El Born's 1714 siege and September 11 National Day, the Bunkers' shantytown and Colònia Güell's
  company-village paternalism all keep the scripts' register.
- **All images owner-supplied → no CREDITS rows.**
- **Scripts:** three header lines on every one (title + `Location:` + `Target length:`; 7 use
  `Coordinates:` + `Location:`). The `_tts-safe` twin proved the header is **not** narrated while the closing
  recommendation line **is** (kept in `transcriptText`). Exactly one `[beat]` per script, 78 total, all
  stripped. Captions extend across sentences to clear 60 chars — **shortest shipped is 67**.
