square is width-limited, so **`--focus` does nothing at all** — the seventh
batch running into this. Re-rendered through a mirror of the tool's own
pipeline (same `trim_bars`, same blur/pad, **same filename**, so `Tours.json`
is untouched):

  *"MUSEUM OF ETHNOGRAPHY"* through the middle. That is the subject's name.

  Delancey vs Rutgers"*, which the centred square had cut in half.

  cropped **the hotel out of the frame entirely**, leaving the presenter
  panel of a split-screen. Now shows the swan sculptures and the lake.
