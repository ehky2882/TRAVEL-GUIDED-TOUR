## The five multi-pin groups, and the stack-cap flag

Every one exists because the owner sent more than one link for the same place. **None was nudged
together to manufacture a group; none was nudged apart to dodge the checker.**

- **Arthur Ashe Stadium ×4** — #1, #3 and #4 name the stadium outright; #2 is a general US Open
  visitor guide whose frame is a match in a stadium bowl.
- **Grove at Grand Bay ×3** · **Bellevue/Lockridge Library ×2** · **Vancouver House ×2** ·
  **Banyan Tree Mayakoba ×2**

### 🔴 Four coincident pins is EXACTLY the Home map's cap

`HomeView.maxStackedPlacecards` is **4** and `TourSetMap.maxStacked` is **3**. So:

- On the **Home map** all four Arthur Ashe pins render and all four are reachable — but there is
  **no headroom**. A fifth US Open pin would put one permanently out of reach, invisibly.
- `TourSetMap`'s cap of 3 is **not binding today**, because each of the four has a *different*
  maker, so no maker page stacks more than one. It would bind on a **place page** or a **list** —
  and a place collapses its members into one capsule pin anyway, which is the Windsor Castle
  lesson: *when coincident pins are being spread to dodge the cap, a place is the real fix.*

**Arthur Ashe Stadium is therefore a genuine place candidate.** Not created — a place needs its own
copy, address, photograph and your approval.

### ⚠️ A separated placement was considered and rejected

I looked at putting #3 (food, and its frame is demonstrably the outdoor plaza with the ESPN
signage, not the stadium interior) on the US Open food village instead. **OSM maps no "South Plaza"
or "Food Village" by name**, and my candidate points reverse-geocoded to unnamed pedestrian ways.
Inventing a coordinate OSM does not name, in order to relieve a cap, is exactly the manufacturing
this catalogue's own rules forbid — so all four sit on the stadium and the cap is flagged instead.

## 🔴 A defect of my own, caught before the commit: `images//`

The first assembly pass shipped **every** hero and stop image URL as
`…/TRAVEL-GUIDED-TOUR/images//file.webp` — a double slash — because the `--image-base` I passed
already ended in one and `build_entry` appends its own (`f"{image_base}/{slug}_hero.webp"`; the
tool's own selftest uses `"https://x/images"`, with no trailing slash).

**Both forms return 200**, so nothing would have been broken — but it is inconsistent with the other
533 pins, and `URLCache` keys on the exact string, so the two spellings cache as separate entries.
**Caught by reading the seed script's SQL output**, not by any check. Reverted, fixed at the
checkpoint, re-assembled; the diff introduces **0** double slashes.
