## 🔴 NOT ONE COORDINATE CAME FROM THE OWNER — every one was derived and then verified

The owner supplied URLs only. Each location was read out of the post's **own
caption**, forward-geocoded, and then **reverse-geocoded at zoom 18** to confirm the
point sits on the venue. Two needed more than that, and both are worth recording
because the reverse-geocode *looked* wrong in each case and was not:

- **Inter&Co Stadium** reverse-geocodes to **"Lymmo"** — downtown Orlando's bus
  circulator, whose route line passes over the point. But the *forward* search
  returns an explicit OSM **`leisure=stadium` relation (9219427) named "Inter.co
  Stadium"**, and 28.54110/−81.38914 is that relation's own centroid. A stadium
  centroid is inside the bowl by definition. **Accepted.**
- **Old Town Kissimmee** reverse-geocodes to **"Sweet Dreams Cafe, Water Street,
  Old Town, *Celebration*"** — a different town. But OSM's own record for the
  enclosing `landuse=retail` area is **"Old Town, house number 5770, Kissimmee,
  34746"**, and the video's caption gives the address as **5770 W Irlo Bronson
  Memorial Hwy, Kissimmee**. Name, house number, city and postcode all agree; the
  "Celebration" is OSM's `place` assignment disagreeing with the postal city, the
  same shape as Stadsarchief Delft sitting in "Den Hoorn". **Accepted, `city:
  "Kissimmee"`.** The hero settles it independently — see below.

**⚠️ Evermore Orlando Resort does not exist in OSM as a named POI**, and a guess
would have been ~4 km out. It IS well mapped in pieces (Evermore Houses, Evermore
Flats, Evermore Way, Evermore Tennis Courts, Evermore Meeting Center). The pin sits
on **Evermore Bay**, the crystal lagoon — which is both the geographic heart of the
resort and literally what the video shows. A bounded viewbox search found it; the
unbounded searches for "Evermore Orlando Resort" all returned nothing at all.

**⚠️ Super Nintendo World has its own OSM node** (`locality`, 28.4386314,
−81.4483461) **566 m from the Epic Universe park node.** The video is about the land,
not the park, so the pin is on the land. Reverse-geocoding it returns "Universal
Epic Universe", which is the correct enclosing feature, not an error.

**⚠️ The UFL video names no venue and no team.** Its caption is `#football #sports
#orlando #thingstodoinorlando #ufl` and the on-screen text is "ORLANDO HAS A FOOTBALL
TEAM". The venue was identified **from the frame** — purple seats and a compact
soccer-specific bowl with an American football field laid over it — then confirmed:
Inter&Co Stadium is Orlando City SC's ground and, since a multi-year partnership
announced 7 October 2025, home of the UFL's **Orlando Storm**, whose first season
kicked off 27 March 2026. The pin is titled for the **venue**, because that is the
place on the map; the team is not asserted anywhere in the entry.

### ⚠️ ONE HERO WAS RE-CROPPED BY HAND, AND THE TOOL CANNOT DO IT

`render_hero` crops the square with `centering=(focus, 0.5)` — **`--focus` moves the
square horizontally and there is no vertical lever.** On the Inter&Co frame (1233×1764)
the centred square starts at row 265 and **sliced "ORLANDO HAS" through the middle of
its letters**, leaving a broken half-line across the top of the pin. Re-rendered with
a **top-weighted square** (`centering=(0.5, 0.0)`), reusing the tool's own `trim_bars`
and the same blur/pad so the two cannot drift; the full sentence now reads and the
whole stadium bowl is still in frame. Same filename, so `Tours.json` is unchanged.

**⚠️ Super Nintendo World is clipped too and was deliberately LEFT ALONE.** Its lost
bottom line is **"Fly Through"** — a video-format label, not the subject's name. The
brand name reads cleanly and the portal is whole. Check what the clipped text actually
says before reaching for a fix.

**A vertical-focus flag is the obvious follow-up** and was deliberately not added
here — it is a change to a 71-check tool in a content batch.
