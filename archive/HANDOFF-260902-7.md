## Reusable mechanics

- **A 45-link batch is a single-pass cached grab**, not 45 tool invocations: fetch each embed to
  disk with the tool's own `Dozent/1.0` UA (~8 s each), then analyse offline. ⚠️ **A browser
  user-agent trips Instagram's challenge page from a datacenter IP** — do not "improve" the UA.
- **Install Pillow first.** `make-link-pin.py --selftest` reports **71/71** with it and **62/62**
  without, and 62/62 reads as a pass and is not one.
- **Call `make-link-pin.py` per URL, not with `--batch`** — `--slug` and `--title` are ignored past
  the first row in batch mode. Checkpoint per pin and retry with backoff; a single CDN blip
  otherwise takes the whole run's state with it.
- **Pass `--image-base` WITHOUT a trailing slash** — `build_entry` appends its own, and the double
  slash is invisible until you read the generated SQL.
