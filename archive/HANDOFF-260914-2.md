## Postscript: the Supabase outage was resolved, and the cause was NOT egress

**Recovered 13:01 UTC, ~11h45m down.** The real cause: the Postgres instance was
**`t4g.nano` — 512 MB RAM, shared CPU** — with memory at 79%, and `get_catalog`
builds the entire catalogue in memory on every call. Fixed by moving compute to
**Micro**, which costs **the same $0.01344/hour** and which Supabase labels a free
upgrade (1 GB, dedicated 2-core).

🔴 **The egress-restriction theory in the body of this handoff was wrong**, as was
the identical guess on the board. The owner had been on **Pro since 10 September**,
four days before the outage. Two sessions inferred the cause from the calendar and
neither asked the one question that would have settled it. 🔴 **Pro raises egress,
not compute** — the plan upgrade left the hardware that caused this untouched.
Both lessons are now in `docs/lessons.md` § 6, with the diagnostic order that
would have found it in minutes.
