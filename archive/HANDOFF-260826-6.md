## 🔴 `Designed by a Master` IS KEPT, NOT REPLACED

Every one of the four pins keeps the generic tag **alongside** its architect name. This looks like
redundancy and is not:

- **`Tag.matches` performs no implication** — a named architect does not imply the generic tag.
- **The curated home shelf is keyed on that literal string** (`Tag.swift`), so stripping it takes
  the tour straight off the shelf built for exactly those tours.
- `CreateTourView` auto-appends it whenever a maker picks an architect, so authored tours behave the
  same way.

**If you are here to tidy the "duplicate" away, that is the change this note exists to stop.**

Verified after the change: **0 named-architect tours are missing the shelf tag**, and **0 of the 327
names are unused** — no dead vocabulary.

## ⚠️ Two naming decisions worth revisiting later

- **`Adjaye Associates` is the practice, not the individual.** That is how the building is credited,
  and how this file already handles `3XN`, `Cobe`, `White Arkitekter` and `Lundgaard & Tranberg`.
  **But the project once rejected `Foster + Partners` as a duplicate of `Norman Foster`**, so the
  practice-vs-person convention is genuinely not uniform. If a future sweep normalises it, this is
  one of the entries to look at.
- **`Richard Rogers` was already in the vocabulary and is a different person** from **James Gamble
  Rogers II** (the Pompidou/Lloyd's architect vs the Winter Park one). Checked on **normalised token
  sets, not strings** — the session-104 rule that stops the vocabulary growing near-duplicates which
  split a shelf in two. **0 collisions** across all four names.
