## 2. 🔴 Two defects were caught before shipping, and one check reported a false pass first

### (a) A hero filename would have overwritten a live file belonging to another open PR

**[#749](https://github.com/ehky2882/TRAVEL-GUIDED-TOUR/pull/749) was open and unmerged**,
carrying 54 pins — **34 of them TikTok `@urbanistariel`, the same creator as this batch**.
Its heroes were already pushed to gh-pages.

Both batches carry a **different post about Tower Bridge**. Both derived the stem
`tower-bridge-urbanistariel_hero.webp`. Uploading mine would have replaced #749's live
bytes at an unchanged URL — which, since #567, **no phone that had downloaded the tour
would ever re-fetch**.

Caught by diffing the 82 stems against the **gh-pages tree** (7,215 `images/` paths, the
listing asserted >1,000 first), not against `main`. Renamed to
`tower-bridge-glass-walkway-urbanistariel_hero.webp`; #749's file untouched.

> **The durable rule:** the handle suffix disambiguates a creator from Atlas, **not a
> creator from themselves**. A batch running beside another must diff hero filenames
> against **gh-pages**, because a parallel batch's heroes land there *before* its PR merges
> and are invisible in `main`.

### (b) A pin nobody could tap

Three posts are the **Museum of Chinese in America**, all on the owner's one Plus Code, and
**`main` already carried a MOCA entry** — depth **4** against `TourSetMap.maxStacked = 3`.
`placecardTours.prefix(3)` drops the rest **silently**: no error, no log, the pin simply
cannot be opened from the map.

`Two Centuries of NYC's Chinatown` moved **405 m** onto OSM's `Chinatown` neighbourhood
node — which is what its caption is actually about (Chinatown's two centuries, told by
MOCA's president). **Nothing is over the cap now; seven clusters sit exactly at 3.**

### (c) ⚠️ The check that found (b) had already returned a false pass

An earlier run of the same neighbour check reported **"zero existing entries within 60 m"**
across all 83 pins. That was wrong. It filtered the catalogue on a top-level `latitude`
field — **tours and pins carry the coordinate on `centroidLatitude` and `stops[0]`, not
there** — so it examined an almost-empty set and reported a clean bill of health.

Re-run against the right field: **32 neighbours**, and the MOCA over-cap group.

> **This is the § "Reading a check's result" failure in a new costume.** Nothing was stale
> and nothing short-circuited; the check ran, on the wrong set. **A "zero findings" result
> against a large corpus is a claim about the corpus — assert the corpus is non-empty
> before believing the verdict**, exactly as the mirror refuses an empty vocabulary parse.
> It was caught only because a *later* check disagreed.

## 3. One post is two pins — and the runbook's open question is now answered

`ZGdQYqKXE` and `ZGdQYxB79` are two different short links resolving to the **same post**,
about the Edinburgh places that inspired J.K. Rowling, with **two different Plus Codes**:

| Code | Reverse-geocodes to | Ships as |
|---|---|---|
| `WRX5+27` | **The Elephant House** (amenity/cafe) | *The Elephant House, Where Harry Potter Began* |
| `WRW4+3G` | War Memorial, Lauriston Place, Quartermile | *George Heriot's School, the Hogwarts Inspiration* |

The second point is on Quartermile, **146 m from OSM's `George Heriot's School`** and
directly across Lauriston Place from it — the vantage you would film the school from, since
it sits behind walls.

**[#757](https://github.com/ehky2882/TRAVEL-GUIDED-TOUR/pull/757) merged mid-session and
its new `docs/link-pin-runbook.md` names this exact case as an open gap** — *"If a batch
needs two pins from one post in one city, there is no convention yet — pick one, write it
here, and say so in the PR."* Both pins are in Edinburgh, so the city fragment cannot
separate them.

**Chosen and written into the runbook: the fragment is the `slug(subject)` — the venue —
when cities collide.** `#elephant-house` and `#george-heriots-school`. City remains the rule
when cities differ, so the city rule is a special case of *name whatever distinguishes the
pins*. The two ad-hoc Charging Bull ids are **deliberately not retro-fitted** — re-minting
live ids would orphan anything saved against them.

⚠️ **The pair shares ONE hero**, so **82 files cover 83 pins** and a
`len(files) == len(pins)` assertion is wrong for this batch. The file is named
`harry-potter-edinburgh-urbanistariel_hero.webp` — for the post, not either subject.

Before minting anything, the scheme was **re-derived from the live catalogue**: 7 of the 10
shared-URL pins reproduce on both tour and stop id from `#slug(city)`, and the three that do
not were brute-forced — `bedi-makky-art-foundry`, `charging-bull`, `naviglio-grande` — which
is what showed the tie-break is a subject slug rather than noise.
