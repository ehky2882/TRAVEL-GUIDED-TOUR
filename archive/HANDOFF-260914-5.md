## 🔴 The finding that matters: PR #885's Tokyo coordinates

A **full 38-pin sweep** of that batch — every venue forward-geocoded by name and
measured against its stored coordinate:

| | count |
|---|---|
| **GROSS (>500 m)** | **3** |
| CHECK (150–500 m) | 3 |
| **NOT-IN-OSM — unverifiable** | **18** |
| ok (<150 m) | 14 |

**The three gross:** Mr. Kanso **4,867 m** · Mizutani Camera **4,339 m** ·
Cafe de l'Ambre **505 m**. The borderline three: Tokyo City Flea Market 379 m,
T.Y. Harbor 318 m, Super Potato 235 m.

**The mechanism, confirmed by reverse-geocoding every shared coordinate.** Small
Tokyo businesses are frequently **not in OpenStreetMap**. Nominatim does not fail
on those — it returns **the precise centroid of the containing block**
(`Kaminarimon 2-chome`, `Hanakawado 1-chome`), or whatever POI it matched on the
street. Three unrelated pins — a coffee house, an Irish pub and a camera shop —
all landed on **one watch shop on Ginza Renga-dori** that way.

🔴 **That centroid carries 6–7 decimal places, so a district fallback is
indistinguishable from a venue hit by inspection.** An earlier note in this
session guessed "rounded district centroid" and was wrong on exactly that point.

⚠️ **The 18 unverifiable are the real exposure — nearly half the batch.** They
were found only because 7 of them *collided*; a venue absent from OSM that sits
alone in its block yields one plausible wrong coordinate and **no signal at
all**. Resolving them needs the **source captions' addresses**, which
`#885`'s own PR body says were the geocoding input — re-geocoding from address
rather than venue name should recover most.

⚠️ **`#893` has since merged 70 MORE Tokyo/Osaka/Kyoto pins, entirely
unchecked**, from the same creator pipeline.

temple**. The batch stored hit `[0]`.

## 🔴 Part-vs-whole is a JUDGEMENT CALL, not a rule

This session first wrote the owner's four decisions into `docs/places.md` as a
settled rule. **They corrected it immediately:** *"I think the part and whole is
a judgement call. I would leave the 3 from my batch."*

Four went one way and three identical-looking cases were deliberately left alone
in the same breath — **Keith Haring's Carmine Street Mural** vs the Tony Dapolito
Recreation Center, **The Blue Ribbon Garden** vs Walt Disney Concert Hall, and
**Hoyt–Schermerhorn Streets Station** vs a pin about the street's name. Those
three stay unmade; do not re-propose them.

`docs/places.md` records this correctly now, and withdraws this session's claim
that the older Big Ben / Bar Luce / Channel Gardens decisions were "inconsistent"
— under a judgement-call framing they are simply other judgements.

**What does hold across all four: name the place for the whole, not the
component.**
