## Judgements worth knowing

- **"Doesn't matter which one" turned out to matter.** The two Casa del Mar thumbnails are not
  alike. The later post is a downward view onto a patio with a palm trunk through the middle and a
  sliver of window wall; the earlier one is the hotel's skylit lobby staircase and reads as the
  building on a map. **A link pin re-hosts only its thumbnail**, so the survivor's frame is the only
  frame that subject will ever have — hence the earlier one.
- **The place's hero was the pin being deleted.** `catalog_places()` filters to ≥ 2 published
  tours, so the page would have stopped being served on its own — but the row would have lingered
  with a hero pointing at a deleted pin's file. `tours.place_id` is `on delete set null`, so the
  survivor unlinks itself.
- **One creator on two platforms is two maker rows.** `YouTube @Thedesigndetourist` and `TikTok
  @thedesigndetourist` are separate uuid5 rows keyed on `<platform>:@handle`. Only the YouTube one
  goes. The SQL asserts the TikTok row still has 19 pins so a mistake rolls back.
- **Two gh-pages heroes are left orphaned** — nothing references them and a deletion push buys
  nothing (the 2026-08-28 pull's precedent).
