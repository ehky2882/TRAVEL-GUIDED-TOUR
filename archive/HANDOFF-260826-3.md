## 🔴 THREE HERO-PIPELINE DEFECTS THIS BATCH WALKED INTO

All three are fixed in `scripts/make-link-pin.py`; selftest **52 → 71**.

1. **`hero_slug()` — the handle suffix is now the tool's job.** #607 applied the convention
   *by hand* because batch mode ignored `--slug` past the first row, and CLAUDE.md said to
   fold it in before the next batch. A bare subject slug is the stem the Atlas tour of the
   same subject already occupies on gh-pages, and since #567 a phone that has downloaded a
   tour would never see the correction. **The handle is appended even to an explicit
   `--slug`** — `--slug` names the subject, and the collision is a property of the filename.

2. **🔴 EVERY YOUTUBE PIN MADE BEFORE THIS HAS BLACK BARS BAKED INTO ITS HERO.**
   YouTube's oEmbed always returns `hqdefault.jpg`, which is 480×360 — a 4:3 canvas with the
   16:9 frame **letterboxed** into it. The square crop kept the bars, and because the app
   shows the middle square of the stored 1200×900, the bands land across the picture. Two
   changes: `best_thumbnail()` prefers **`maxresdefault.jpg`** (the same frame at 1280×720,
   nothing to trim, 2.7× the pixels) and falls back when absent; `trim_bars()` strips uniform
   dark borders off whatever arrives.
   ⚠️ **`trim_bars` is deliberately conservative and must stay that way:** a strip counts as a
   bar only if it is both **dark AND near-uniform**, at most **30%** comes off any one edge,
   and an implausible trim returns the original — otherwise the fix starts eating night
   photographs. Pinned by a test that feeds it a dark *textured* image and asserts no crop.
   **⚠️ NOT BACKFILLED. The 29 pre-existing link pins still carry their old heroes**, and
   correcting one means a NEW filename (`..._hero-2.webp`), never an overwrite — #567.

3. **`--title` and `--focus` — human judgement the tool cannot infer.**
   An awards channel titles every upload to a house pattern (*"BRICK AWARD 26 Winner Category
   Sharing public spaces - Dạo Mẫu (Mothergoddess) Museum & Temple, VN"* — award, category, a
   country code, and a misspelling of the place) and composes its thumbnails as a **photograph
   beside a text panel**, so a centred square lands on the seam and slices the lettering
   mid-word. `--title` names the subject; `--focus left|center|right` picks which square
   survives. **The source's own words are kept verbatim in `longDescription` either way**, and
   the id is uuid5 over `sourceURL`, so neither flag can change it (asserted by a test).

**The per-pin focus used, so a re-run reproduces:** `left` for Đạo Mẫu and Clase Azul,
`right` for Maya Somaiya and Terra Cotta, `center` for the other five.
