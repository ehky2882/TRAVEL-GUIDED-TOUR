**Proven by measurement rather than asserted:** `--pins` goes **557 images with 7 groups → 550 with
none**, the difference being exactly the phantom entries collapsing. Each corrected string was
**asserted equal to that pin's own hero before it was written**, so the fix cannot have invented a
