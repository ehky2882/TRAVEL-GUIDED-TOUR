## 🔴 My Instagram parser was wrong, not the data

The first parse reported `handle=None`, no caption and no thumbnail for all 15 Instagram posts —
while the pages were plainly healthy at 258–267 KB. **`contextJSON` is a JSON-ENCODED STRING**,
so everything inside it is escaped (`\"username\"`), and a regex looking for raw `"username":"`
finds nothing. The fix is to extract the string value, `json.loads` it once to unescape, then
`json.loads` the result. **A parse that returns nothing on a healthy page is the parser's fault
until proven otherwise.**
