## 🔴 An Instagram STORY HIGHLIGHT can never be a link pin

Two of the sixteen were `instagram.com/s/<token>?story_media_id=…`. **Three independent
blocks, all structural:**

1. **`LinkSource.embedURL` (`Models/Tour.swift`) matches only `p`, `reel`, `tv`** — a `/s/`
   path returns `nil`, so the app can build **no player**. Verified in the Swift itself, not
   just the tool's mirror. Same class as a TikTok `/photo/` carousel.
2. **No `display_url`** → no hero, and a pin with no hero cannot exist.
3. **No caption blob** → no subject, no description.
