## 2. 🔴 The Atlas tour could never fire at its own subject

Its coordinate was `40.8098, -73.9632`, which **reverse-geocodes to 3019 Broadway** — 243 m from
the church, outside its polygon, and on the wrong street.

**It is geofenced at 60 m.** So standing at Riverside Church did nothing: no error, no dead link,
no log line — the tour simply never started. The Chelsea Hotel and IAC Building class exactly.

**It is not a deliberate vantage.** The tour's own transcript opens:

> *"You're on Riverside Drive at 120th Street, outside Riverside Church"*

and its caption reads *"Riverside Drive at 120th Street, Morningside Heights"*. Broadway is neither.
That distinction is what separates this from **Grand Central** and **Petersen**, where the tour's
offset coordinate *was* the vantage its script named and the place was anchored on the tour.

## 5. 🔴 A real gap in the Python validator mirror — found by the fault suite, fixed

The first fault run came back **11/12**, missing `triggerMode: "geofence"`.

**Cause:** the Swift validator declares `enum StopTriggerMode: String` and `let triggerMode:
StopTriggerMode`, so a bad value **fails its Codable decode**. A raw-JSON mirror sees nothing
unless it checks the domain explicitly — and mine checked **no enum domains at all**. That left it
blind to exactly the class of defect CLAUDE.md records shipping before: `triggerMode geofence →
geofenced` across 18 tours.

It also never checked `primaryCategory` — a second gap in the same place.

**Fixed:** the mirror now parses `StopTriggerMode`, `TourKind` and `TourCategory` **from the Swift
validator, never retyped**, and **refuses to run on a short parse** — the same discipline it already
applied to the tag vocabulary. **11/12 → 12/12**, and it still reports **0 errors** on the real
catalogue, which independently proves no existing tour carries a bad enum value.

⚠️ **The mirror lives in `/tmp`, not the repo. Fold this into any future mirror.**
