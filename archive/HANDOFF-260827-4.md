## ⚠️ Tooling gap found: `check-image-duplicates.py` cannot scope to a link-pin batch

It takes `--maker <CODE>` (a city) or `--all` (5,800+ images, every batch). **A link-pin batch has
no maker code** — its creators are individual pinned accounts — so there is no cheap way to run
the repo's own checker over just the pins that were added. `--file` alone is rejected.

Covered here by running the same two-stage check by hand over the exact 23 heroes (SHA-256 for
byte-identity, then a 32×32 thumbnail comparison for the perceptual stage), and the live bytes
were then confirmed to match those exact files. **The obvious fix is a `--since <ref>` or
`--pins` flag**; every link-pin batch has hit this and worked around it silently.
